Stepcraft 840

Manufacturer reference only — not the FabLab Winti machine pinout. The STEPCRAFT SC2 v5.1 manual covers the 840 size and gives unit-control/optional-module schedules for X1, X2 and optional 4th-axis X101. The Winti page does not identify the installed generation, controller or fitted options. The tables preserve each manual position as a machine-side reference peripheral; they do not establish fitment, connector orientation, shell/gender, electrical compatibility or a cable. X1 is named only as a 25-position LPT adapter; DB-25 shell/gender is unverified. Every contact remains OPEN pending route review. STEPCRAFT SC2 v5.1 manual, §6.5; FabLab Winti source page.
