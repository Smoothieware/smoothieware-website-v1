Tantillus portable RepRap printer

Identity

Tantillus is a compact, portable XY-head RepRap design. Its wiki records a GPLv3 license and a working release status; the external project domain is reported as no longer reliable, so this dossier relies on RepRap-hosted material only.

Wiki evidence

  • RepRap Tantillus: design history, status, specifications, case assembly, electronics section and CAD repository reference.
  • RepRap printer-by-picture index: model image and 100 × 100 × 100 mm class dimensions.

Operation and construction

The wiki describes a portable, self-replicating mini printer and records case-printing/assembly notes, including failures with ABS warping and successful PLA parts. A cable-driven Z variant is noted. The article contains separate electronics and assembly sections, but the surfaced excerpt did not include a complete wiring table; follow the revision-specific wiki build manual before assembling.

Pinout and visuals

No verified connector pinout is transcribed from the captured excerpt. The source has exploded-view and case imagery, which document mechanical assembly rather than electrical contacts.

Uncertainty

The wiki page contains multiple historical updates. Match the chosen case and Z-axis revision to its corresponding wiring before use.
