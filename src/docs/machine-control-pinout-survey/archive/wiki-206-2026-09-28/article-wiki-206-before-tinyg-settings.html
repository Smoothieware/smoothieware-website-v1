Complete wiki-researched machine dossier

Shapeoko 2 CNC router

Identity

RaumZeitLabor's wiki has a dedicated “Shapeoko 2” machine page. This exact model identity is distinct from Shapeoko 3 and later generations.

Wiki evidence

  • RaumZeitLabor Shapeoko 2 page: machine-specific discussion of CNC routing, 2.5D work and its GRBL-era control context.
  • RaumZeitLabor main page: lists Shapeoko 2 among the lab's devices.

Operation and visuals

The wiki gives an active-machine workflow and says to plan carefully because cutting failures can damage the work or injure the operator. It names materials tested on this installation (HDPE, plywood, aluminum and Delrin) and gives source-specific feed/pass examples; those are historical shop values, not universal settings. The page documents a TinyG controller, 115200 baud 8N1 with hardware flow control, 260 × 275 × 70 mm working area, 300 W spindle, eight limit switches and a machine E-stop. The page contains connector photographs; its pin table is transcribed below.

Pinout

The wiki numbers the drag-chain motor cable’s printed cores, not motor connector cavity pins. Its general order is 1 red B1/B+, 2 blue B2/B−, 3 green A1/A− and 4 black A2/A+. The separate X-stepper exception is 1 green A1, 2 red B1, 3 black A2, 4 blue B2. The source says green and black swap on the left Y stepper, but does not separately tabulate that motor’s resulting core/contact assignment. Neither order establishes a universal coil-colour convention or a selected SmoothieBox motor-driver route. Its sensor connector map follows:

Limits

The page is historical and does not establish that the installation remains active. It names the TinyG controller, 300 W spindle and working area, but does not establish current hardware, complete settings or safety-interlock implementation.
