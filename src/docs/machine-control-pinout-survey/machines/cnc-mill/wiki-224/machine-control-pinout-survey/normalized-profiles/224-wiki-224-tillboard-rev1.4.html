Reference-only schedule. This is the author schematic contact schedule for the MPCNC Nano Estlcam Shield Rev 1.4, not evidence that this board is installed in the local VF1. Sorotec lists the controller, motors, reference sensors/cables and spindle among items excluded from its VF1 kit. Every listed Tillboard contact is OPEN to SmoothieBox. Dotted = GUESS in the legend; no routes are guessed here. J2:3 alone has an explicit source NoConn mark.

29 separate J connectors · 94 numbered positions. J3 and J15 contribute 12 board service-header positions; the other 82 are reference connector positions. Schematic pin order is not a mating-face view. No DB25 appears in the cited board schematic.
