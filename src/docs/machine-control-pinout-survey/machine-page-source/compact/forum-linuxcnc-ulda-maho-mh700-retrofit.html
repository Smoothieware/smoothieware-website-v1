ulda's Maho MH700 LinuxCNC retrofit

Identification lead

LinuxCNC Forum member ulda reports a successful retrofit of a four-axis Maho 700 milling machine. The original 8080 processor control was removed after recurring errors, along with the old Philips glass scales. The owner retained the DC motor amplifiers, installed new glass scales, and rewired the machine to a Motenc-100 motion controller.
