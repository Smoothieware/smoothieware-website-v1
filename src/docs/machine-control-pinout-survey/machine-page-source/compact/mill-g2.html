The 2022 V2 manual identifies a 32-bit/TB67S109 former controller, three four-wire motor sockets, paired X/Y/Z limit sockets, red+/black− spindle motor leads, probe, EStop and offline accessory. A SainSmart-hosted review reports only three of the six limit sockets occupied on its machine, with each axis’s two switches wired in parallel; it does not identify which socket of each pair is used across production.

Why this controller: Original motion-driver electronics are on the board being replaced. Use Prime integrated phase outputs directly to measured winding pairs; do not connect STEP/DIR signal outputs to motor windings.
