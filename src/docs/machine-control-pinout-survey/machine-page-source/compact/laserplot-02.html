Contact assignments

The manufacturer Type 2 guide shows J3 as the low-voltage harness, J6 as Y motor output, non-ribbon J7 as X motor output and J5 as the limit-switch plug; a ribbon K40 uses J4 for X and limits. A separate J1 PWM output lead goes to the Type 2 PSU’s middle IN terminal in its G/IN/5V bank. This is a source-scoped Type 2 reference, not an installed harness or SmoothieBox route.

Why this controller: Original motion-driver electronics are on the board being replaced. Use Prime integrated phase outputs directly to measured winding pairs; do not connect STEP/DIR signal outputs to motor windings. Confirm installed board, motor current and power rating from the cited machine source.
