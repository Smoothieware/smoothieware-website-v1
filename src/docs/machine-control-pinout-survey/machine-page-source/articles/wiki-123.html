Charmhigh CHM-T36VA pick-and-place machine

Evidence status: London Hackspace lists the exact model as working and training-required in a 2022 entry. Separate CHMT36VA community sources describe an STM32F407 controller, rear nine-pin RS-232 and USB-B camera-capture connections, and the machine subsystems below; installed revision and firmware are unverified.

Identity and status

London Hackspace lists “Charmhigh CHM-T36VA Pick-and-Place Machine” in its Fabrication inventory. The inventory says it is located in the Murder Kitchen, working, and training-required. The wiki entry was last updated 12 May 2022; that date means the status is historical and should not be treated as a live machine check.

Model-specific interface evidence

OpenPnP community material describes X/Y closed-loop steppers and encoders, Z lift, two nozzle-rotation steppers, feeder axes, X/Y home switches, a Z optical sensor, vacuum/blower/drag-pin actuators, cameras, lights and a buzzer. It identifies a rear nine-pin RS-232 connector and a USB-B port routed to the camera capture card. Charmhigh’s CHM-T36VA User Manual separately calls for a computer-side USB-to-serial cable and a USB camera cable, but gives no contact schedule. A separate community firmware repository maps logical STM32F407 GPIOs to functions but says official schematics are unavailable; those MCU assignments are not external harness contacts. The SVG lists DE-9 positions 1–9 as function OPEN and USB-B standard contacts 1–4; neither has a SmoothieBox route.

Operation and wiring limits

No verified external driver, sensor, actuator, or serial connector pin assignment is available for this installed unit. No polarity, electrical ratings, returns, mating view, or safety wiring is inferred. A direct SmoothieBox connection is not established.
