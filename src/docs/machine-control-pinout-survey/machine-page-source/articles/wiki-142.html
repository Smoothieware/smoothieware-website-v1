Eventorbot RepRap printer

Identity

Eventorbot is an open-source printer design with a single steel frame. The RepRap wiki describes its goal as a rigid, low-cost machine with many printable parts.

Wiki evidence

  • RepRap Eventorbot: dimensions, parts list, drawings, upgrades, videos and assembly sequence.
  • RepRap machine index: model catalog.

Specifications and operation

The wiki lists a 152 × 152 × 152 mm print volume, 37 printed parts and 1.5 mm sheet steel frame stock. It links engineering drawings and sequential tutorials covering frame, X, Z, extruder and Y assembly. The wiki also describes a dual-extrusion upgrade. These are design-level notes, not verification of a physical machine.

Electrical parts in the Eventorbot design BOM

The RepRap Eventorbot 1.2 parts list names three four-wire NEMA 17 motors, a geared PG35L-048 extruder stepper, four push-button momentary endstop switches, a panel-mount 5.5 × 2.1 mm DC jack, and a 12 V / 5 A supply (12 A if the optional heated bed is used). Its hotend list includes a 5.6 Ω / 5 W resistor. It also lists a Sanguinololu 1.3a motherboard with stepper drivers. These are source design facts, not proof of a particular machine's installed parts.

Sanguinololu 1.3a reference contacts

The RepRap Sanguinololu page describes X/Y/Z/extruder driver sockets and three-position endstop headers. The outside endstop positions are GND and SIG (the page's prose does not say which is left or right); the center position supplies selectable 5 V or source voltage through the Stop Volt solder link. Its revision history says 1.3b2 changed the three-position endstop connector to a four-position CD-ROM form, which is not substituted here. Four 16-position driver-carrier socket schedules are retained as board-reference positions with functions unassigned. None establishes that a Sanguinololu board is fitted locally.

Contact and route limits

The source-counted four-wire motor leads are editorial conductor slots, not connector cavities. The extruder motor's lead map, endstop switch terminals, jack contact count/polarity, exact power option and complete harness census are not published. Every candidate remains OPEN; no SmoothieBox route or dotted guess is justified by these sources.

Limits

The performance claims about vibration are design rationale, not comparative test evidence. No local machine serial, controller, motor, harness, or wiring installation has been verified.
