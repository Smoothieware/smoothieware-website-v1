Complete machine research dossier

FoldaRap 4.0

Identity

FoldaRap 4.0 is a foldable, portable RepRap printer design. The maker repository distinguishes its winter 2017 release from the separate FoldaRap 3.5 release. The cited design options do not prove a particular machine's fitted electronics.

Generation-specific electronics evidence

The maker's Item F 4.0 bill-of-materials column identifies five stepper motors (X, Y, two Z positions, and extruder), X/Y sensor microswitches with separate two-pin sensor leads, one two-pin fan lead, an E3D Lite6 hotend, two small fans, and a 90 W bed film with a 100 kΩ probe. The wiki describes external 12 V/24 V and internal PSU options. The Item F 4.0 BOM assigns quantity one to external 12 V 138 W; external 24 V 160 W entries have no quantity. The repository also contains Minitronics, Melzi, and Duet 2 WiFi configurations; none establishes the fitted controller or machine contact map.

Contact and route limits

  • The X/Y sensor leads each have two individually listed, unnumbered harness positions. A dotted path to the corresponding SmoothieBox MIN SIGNAL/GND pair is a functional guess only. Switch-terminal choice, conductor order, polarity/logic, board fitment, and electrical compatibility remain unverified. SENSOR + is left open.
  • Motor contact positions and coil pairs, fan contacts, heater/thermistor positions, bed contacts, and PSU connector positions are not mapped by these sources. Their SmoothieBox routes remain OPEN.
  • The maker BOM lists a rear-plate Kycon KPJX-PM-4S jack and separately an unquantified shielded KPJX-PM-4S-S option. The exterior diagram lists manufacturer contacts 1, 2, 3, and 4 as individual reference positions, all FoldaRap functions and routes OPEN; only the shielded option adds shield tabs A and B, also individually shown and OPEN. No jack polarity or role is assigned.
  • The Item F 4.0 BOM also lists one EU power cord, one LCD specifically for Minitronics, and one USB cable. Their connector types and contact schedules are not documented; the diagram names each peripheral without inventing pin counts or routes.
  • SmoothieBox MOTOR CONTROL STEP/DIR/ENABLE pins are logic outputs for an external driver; do not connect motor windings directly. BED SWITCH CONTROL is not a bed-heater output: its cited Q3 stage is rated 60 V / 0.3 A, far below a direct 90 W heater load.
  • No inspected FoldaRap 4.0 source identifies a DB25 connector.
