CNC Automation M3X mill

Identity

Artisans Asylum calls the machine “M3X CNC Milling Machine,” maker CNC Automation, model M3X, serial 041721. The wiki notes that the nameplate says “Accu II,” while the machine's functional name is M3X; preserve both identifiers.

The owner page, retrieved for this review on 2026-09-27, describes three-axis table motion, servos and a CNC Automation control system driven by a DOS computer in the rear power box. This is source-reported architecture, not a confirmed controller-board model, fitted revision or STEP/DIR input contract. The owner page itself displays a 2025-01-21 last-updated date. [A1]

Operation

The wiki describes G-code generated by CAD/CAM, optional simulation, then machining a vise-held metal workpiece. The spindle is raised/lowered and tooling changed manually. Startup involves checking the E-stop, energizing the power box, booting the DOS computer and loading a program from USB. The listed shutdown options include the front E-stop or saving/quitting before powering down. Training is required; automatic movement can start unexpectedly.

This paragraph records the owner's operating description, not a retrofit procedure or a safety-circuit schematic. Manual spindle raising/lowering and tool changing do not establish spindle-control connector functions. [A1]

Pinout and diagrams

The reviewed evidence establishes named computer I/O ports but no installed motion-controller connector pinout. The two DB25 housings are separate external machine-side peripherals: LPT1 is not COM2. No numbered PC I/O contact is promoted to an M3X axis, servo, limit, enable, return or E-stop assignment. [A1, A2]

The primary drawing includes five peripheral groups: LPT1, COM2, COM1, USB program loading and the front E-stop. Only the three named D-sub forms have individually numbered positions. USB and E-stop have OPEN group boundaries because their contact count, numbering and electrical contract are not established by the reviewed evidence. The reported floppy bay, DOS control system, servos and manual spindle/tool operation are contextual hardware, not additional selected wiring endpoints. [A1, A2]

Rendering and count semantics

The existing proposed Chapter 18 carrier schedule remains 24 exterior banks / 82 individually labeled terminals, plus four separate unnumbered service ports. Carrier terminal ordinals are not Core board pin numbers or mating-face positions. The primary M3X drawing has five peripheral groups, 59 enumerated positions, 25 generic PC-LPT source positions, 34 serial form-only positions, zero documented SmoothieBox conductors, zero dotted guesses and 59 unrouted numbered positions. Two additional unnumbered groups, USB and E-stop, have OPEN boundaries; they contribute no invented contact count.

The catalog's source flag on the 25 LPT rows records a fact in K1 only. The 34 serial rows use form_inventory_only, which the renderer already displays and counts separately. A one-line validator extension permits that existing fact category in reference-catalog rows. All three D-sub cards remain reference-layer peripherals outside SmoothieBox. OPEN means no selected route, not electrically disconnected, NC or unused. The renderer's reference_contacts count includes both reference functions and form-only rows and must not be presented as 59 electrically documented M3X pins.
