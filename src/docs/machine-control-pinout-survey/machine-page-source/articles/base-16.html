Documented evidence · unresolved wiring

Documented / version scope: Manufacturer Rev 9.3 knowledge article explicitly lists DB25 output-capable pins 1–9, 14, 16, 17; dedicated inputs 10–13, 15; +5 V TTL signal domain; three relays; 0–10 V spindle analog converter. Input pins each have 4.7 kΩ pull-up/pull-down jumpers. Pin 14 PWM and 16 DIR affect two spindle relays according to US/INT jumpers J10/J11; relay 3 follows pin 1 or 17 selected by J12. Caution: relay/analog outputs are C11G functions; a V2-Core retrofit would need its own compatible 0–10 V and isolation path, not a direct “same pin number” assumption.
