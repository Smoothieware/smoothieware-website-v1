Documented evidence · source-revision contacts

Documented: The TwoTrees TTS-55 Pro board page and shared board image show X, dual-Y, X/Y-limit and laser connector locations. The Makerbase V2.1_002 schematic numbers the 21 current figure positions and the PCB drawing locates them. The manufacturer-authored TTS-55/10 Pro manual mirror specifies 12 V 4 A machine input. Unresolved: These sources do not prove the fitted board revision, motor-phase harness, limit polarity, module-side laser cavities, cable continuity or physical SmoothieBox route. J27 is a Z board option, not a proven installed motor. Schematic net +12V is input-fed, not proof of a regulated 12 V laser output on 24 V models; former motor-driver outputs are not STEP/DIR inputs.
