RepRap 1.0 Darwin

Identity

Darwin is the first RepRap rapid-prototyping machine design. It is an open, self-replicating FFF printer design, not a commercial product model.

Wiki evidence

  • RepRap Darwin: design history and legacy status.
  • RepRap 1.0 Darwin build overview: mechanism and nominal specifications.
  • RepRap-hosted Darwin figure: axis-labelled Cartesian overview; the wiki-hosted figure shows the XY carriage, Z bed, axes and nominal frame dimensions.

Machine and operation

The wiki describes an XY moving deposition head and a Z-moving bed, driven by steppers. Two fixed extruders are described for build and support material. Nominal working volume is 230 × 230 × 100 mm; the design is adjustable. The build instructions are linked from the wiki. The source gives USB computer interfacing and 12 V DC power, but not a universal controller board.

Pinout and diagram transcription

No universal Darwin connector schedule is established because the wiki describes a design family without binding a particular build to one electronics revision. The pinned RepRap Stepper Motor Controller Card v1.2 page, revision 151930, documents the P9 four-way 2.54 mm-pitch motor socket and source-numbered contact order. The table above transcribes all twelve X/Y/Z socket positions. X order is reversed relative to Y/Z in the source. These are motor winding outputs, not motion-controller STEP/DIR/ENABLE inputs. The card page separately lists PIC package pins; those are not connector contacts and are excluded. The machine figure labels axes and mechanism only; it does not map them to electrical contacts.

Limits

Darwin is retained for historical reference. Build volume, electronics and wiring vary by build.
