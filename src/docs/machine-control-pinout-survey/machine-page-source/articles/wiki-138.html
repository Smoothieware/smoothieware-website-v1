Elegoo Mars 2P

Research status: wiki-sourced resin-printer model dossier. Captured 2026-09-23.

Wiki-recorded configuration

The SoMakeIt wiki names the unit “Elegoo Mars 2P” and lists a 129 × 80 × 160 mm build volume, LCD/FEP bed system, and 0.05 mm XY resolution (1620 × 2560). It is for liquid resin. The wiki names Chitubox on the space PC for slicing and a USB stick for job transfer; it lists the unit as functional and notes a wash/cure station.

Wiki-recorded use and handling

The linked printer page directs readers to the space’s printing instructions. The wiki’s status and configuration are community-maintained. Resin-specific PPE and disposal details were not present in the inspected model entry, so this dossier does not invent them.

Connections and pinout

The wiki specifies USB-stick job transfer, but does not identify the USB connector generation or publish a controller-board, LCD, exposure, motor, sensor, or power pinout. No electrical contact map is available in the cited entry.
