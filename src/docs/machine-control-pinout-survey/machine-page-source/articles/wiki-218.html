Complete wiki-researched machine dossier

Thunder Laser Nova 63 — Dallas Makerspace

Research capture: 2026-09-23. Dallas Makerspace's laser wiki names its large-format unit “Big Thunder,” a Thunder Nova 63.

Wiki-recorded configuration and operation

The wiki lists a 1600 × 1000 mm work area and 130 W output. Its general Thunder Nova notes state that Nova 35 and Nova 63 machines use servo systems described as steppers with optical encoders. The page warns that detected skipped steps can make a machine stop tracking its position and crash the head; it says Reset forces a re-home. It notes the same loss-of-position issue may occur with the rotary attachment. The community wiki requires its Laser Basics certification class for Thunder lasers and describes the curriculum as covering safety, RDWorks, and Thunder operation.

The same page identifies two separate Nova 35 units, Donner and Blitzen. They are not counted as separate model identities in this dossier, and this Nova 63 dossier is kept distinct from the existing Nova 35 entry. The generic servo and reset guidance applies to both families per the wiki; the page does not establish Nova-63-specific controller parameters or wiring.

Manufacturer Nova Series family-manual reference and contact limits

The Thunder Laser Nova Series Unified User Manual covers Nova 24, 35, 51 and 63. Its machine-view text names PC USB, U-disk USB and Ethernet ports, and locates TL-Timer hardware inside the Nova 35/51/63 enclosure. It also names laser supply and chiller power sockets, exhaust and blow-air ports, Water IN/OUT, and a No-Water protection signal port. The Dallas page does not identify its installed controller, timer revision, chiller configuration, or connector pinouts.

The manual's TL-Timer section (printed pp. 57–58) individually names OUT1–OUT8, 5V Output 1, 5V Output 2, Upgrade, 24V Input, and In1–In8. Their source-listed functions are shown in the linked detailed schedule. These are interface/function IDs, not physical terminal positions; the manual does not provide terminal cavity counts or individual pin signal/polarity assignments for these groups. All 20 references remain OPEN. Manual-designated spare interfaces are not relabeled as electrical NC.

The 2024 unified manual is kept separate from older Nova-35 timer schedules. No mains, laser-power, heater, exhaust, air-assist or safety wiring is proposed. Sources: Thunder Laser Nova Series Unified User Manual; Nova Series Wiring Diagram TL Timer V8.4.
