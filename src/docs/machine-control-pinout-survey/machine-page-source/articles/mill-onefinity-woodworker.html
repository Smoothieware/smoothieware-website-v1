Interface reading

Signal domains: Motion control or motor phases, limits/probe, spindle and safety where fitted. Controller family: Buildbotics-derived.

What is still missing

The generic Buildbotics table must be checked against the Onefinity fork/board revision and installed DB25 adapter before any label.

Documented evidence · unresolved wiring

Documented: Onefinity official packing forum post, axis-to-controller motor output map M0 X, M1 Y, M2 Y, M3 Z; Buildbotics manual v1.0, 25-pin I/O and motor port sections, old 12–36 V hardware. M ports are motor phase outputs, not step/dir; manual gives four-contact motor layout upper-left B+, lower-left B−, lower-right A+, upper-right A−. Old manual DB25 pin 6 and pin 20 are +3.3 V, pin 17 PWM, pin 15 enable, pin 16 direction, 22 probe, 23 E-stop. New Buildbotics manual v2.0 gives pin 6 0–10 V on 24–48 V controller; never transfer to Gen1 without board identification. Buildbotics I/O breakout warns M and M2 variants have different pinouts. Unresolved: Onefinity firmware/hardware revision, motor connector face and E-stop safety; v1.0 E-stop is software controlled, not certified.
