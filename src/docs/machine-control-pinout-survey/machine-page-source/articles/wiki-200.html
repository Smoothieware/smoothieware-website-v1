RepRap Wallace

Identity

Wallace is a parametric open-source Cartesian printer design described by the RepRap wiki as a robust redesign based on the Printrbot concept. Do not conflate it with Wally.

Wiki evidence

  • RepRap Wallace: design description, mechanism, dimensions and build-guide link.
  • Wallace build manual: assembly sections and example rod dimensions.
  • RepRap printer-by-picture index: visual catalog entry and comparison table.

Specifications and operation

The wiki says the design uses 6 mm smooth/threaded rods and NEMA 14 motors by default, with parametric alternatives. It reports a possible 200 × 200 × 140 mm build volume, 26 printed parts and approximately 0.07 mm X/Y and 0.025 mm Z positioning increments under the described microstepping. The manual covers X, Y, Z and bed assembly. The page is marked experimental/work-in-progress.

Pinout and visuals

The pinned Wallace design page labels electronics as a tentative parts-list category, and the mechanical build manual ends by directing the builder to wire electronics separately. Neither source names an installed or required controller revision or assigns motor, endstop, power, heater, thermistor or signal connector contacts. The printer-by-picture page is a comparison index, not a wiring schedule. No machine contacts or SmoothieBox routes are inferred.

Wiki sources

RepRap sources describe an experimental mechanical design and do not provide its electronics map.
