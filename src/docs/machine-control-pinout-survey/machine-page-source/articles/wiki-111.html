Adapto RepRap printer

Identity

Adapto is a configurable RepRap printer design. Its base-design BOM identifies five mechanical motor locations (X, Y, two Z, and extruder E), while its electronics note requests four stepper channels and two heaters. These are design-level requirements; the installed controller, connectors, contacts, and harness are not identified.

Wiki evidence

  • RepRap Adapto: component lengths, frame assembly, mechanical construction and revision caveats.
  • Adapto Flex: a separate experimental toolhead-focused variant, linked here only for distinction.
  • RepRap machine list: design index.

Construction and operation

The guide gives an example using 20 × 20 mm extrusion, with 300 mm X, 340 mm Y and 420 mm Z frame members; it notes approximate lost travel from frame to usable travel. Its BOM identifies X, Y, two Z, and extruder E motor locations and lists a hot end plus heated bed. It cautions that parts may differ and upgrades may be missing.

Pinout and visuals

The base Adapto build guide gives a four-stepper/two-heater controller requirement but does not identify the installed board, wiring, or connector pinout. Its BOM lists one STL containing three endstop-holder parts; the upstream repository README separately lists the X-axis holder as a todo, so availability varies by revision. Neither source proves fitted switches. The linked Adapto Flex is a separate variant; its SmoothieBoard suggestion is not evidence for this base profile.

Limits

The cited build information is user-contributed and under construction. Confirm the selected frame dimensions, design revision, controller, harness, and fitted switch/heater configuration before wiring.

Wiki sources

The base-design RepRap wiki supports design-level motor/heater requirements and revision-dependent endstop-holder evidence only. No controller or connector contacts are specified; Adapto Flex is a separate variant.
