What is still missing

The published 440 path runs through DB25 J4/J10 and IDC26 to the cabinet board/MX3660. Pin 10 is a limit/door input and pin 15 is E-stop reset; neither proves the cabinet's safety circuit. Pins 18–25 and electrical ratings remain unresolved. Tormach 440 electrical schematic index.
