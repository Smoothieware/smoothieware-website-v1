T-962 solder reflow oven — Bristol Hackspace

Wiki evidence status: exact oven model with a local modification and operating page. The modification revision and current firmware version are not stated.

Identity and configuration

Bristol Hackspace identifies this as a T-962 solder reflow oven and gives an effective soldering area of 180 × 235 mm. Its local unit differs from an untouched factory configuration: the cardboard insulation was removed and replaced with aluminium tape, a cold-junction temperature sensor was added, and the firmware was flashed. The wiki links the firmware repository, but this dossier uses only the wiki's description and does not infer a board revision or firmware build.

Operation

The local instructions say to place the oven on a heat-proof mat, power it on, and put the PCB on the rack. Press F4 to open profile selection, use F1/F2 to select a profile, press S to choose it, then press S to start. Manual mode opens with F3; F1/F2 set temperature, F3/F4 set a timer, and the countdown begins after preheating. When the timer expires, the oven begins cooling.

Electrical connections and diagrams

The wiki page does not provide a connector pinout, wiring diagram, temperature-sensor terminal assignment, or a detailed controller-board identity. No wiring map is inferred from the T-962 model name. The page contains no machine-specific diagram transcription beyond the stated rack and interface-key procedure.

Safety

The wiki warns that the PCB and oven underside may remain hot after reflow, including during the cooling phase. Use heat-resistant placement and handle the board only after it has cooled.
