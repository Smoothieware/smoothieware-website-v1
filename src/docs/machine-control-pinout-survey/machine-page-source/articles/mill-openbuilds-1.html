Documented evidence · unresolved wiring

Documented: OpenBuilds BlackBox X32 layout, official indexed documentation (source page unavailable): 24 V supply, four onboard stepper motor outputs, limit/probe/door, tool PWM 0–3.3 V, enable 0/3.3 V, separate 0–10 V analog and relay functions; VFD wiring makes 0–10 V a low-level VFD command with separate Relay1 enable and Relay2 direction. Unresolved: exact MiniMill machine wiring, motor wire color, installed X32 revision and accessible schematic. X32 pairing may be a configuration, not factory-standard MiniMill.

2026-09-26 X32 reference update: The OpenBuilds X32 NEMA23 guide and official connection drawing mark four A+/A−/B+/B− motor-driver outputs and four GND/V+/SIG limit/probe inputs. The Xtension limit guide documents the three-core controller input pattern. These 28 screw marks are optional former-controller references, not evidence that this MiniMill has an installed X32, OpenBuilds motor colours, fitted limit/probe leads or matched SmoothieBox electrical interfaces. The drawing-order indexes are not manufacturer-stamped cavity numbers. All 28 remain OPEN; no physical SmoothieBox route is selected.
