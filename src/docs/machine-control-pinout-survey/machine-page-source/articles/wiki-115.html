Source-reviewed machine interface dossier

Bambu Lab X1 Carbon

Evidence scope: The exact-model X1 Carbon Quick Start Guide and Bambu manufacturer replacement-part listings establish named interfaces and component-level context. Appropedia supplies catalogue identity only. The sources do not establish a particular machine's fitted board/cable revision, a complete connector schedule, or any SmoothieBox connection.

Official X1 Carbon guide

The guide (document Y.BC.BQ.Z0052-10) labels the toolhead, 5-inch touchscreen/LCD cable, chamber camera, auxiliary cooling fan, four-pin Bambu Bus port and power socket. It also names Micro SD/eMMC storage, Wi-Fi/Bambu Bus connectivity, a Dual-Core Cortex M4 motion controller and a Quad ARM A7 application processor. These are component and function labels, not a pinout.

The guide lists 100–240 VAC, 50/60 Hz input, maximum power of 1000 W at 220 V or 350 W at 110 V, and USB output of 5 V / 1.5 A. It names five closed-loop fan functions—Part Cooling, Hot End, Control Board, Chamber Temperature Regulator and Auxiliary Part Cooling—and onboard sensor context, but publishes no fan connector counts or per-contact roles. The mains socket is context only, not a SmoothieBox hookup.

OPEN contact inventory and cable context

The current reference overlay contains 16 editorially indexed OPEN positions: six at heatbed signal cable end A, six at end B, and four at the rear Bambu Bus port. The guide supports the four-position Bambu Bus count. The heatbed listing describes a double-ended six-contact 1.25 mm cable, 555 ± 5 mm long, for hotbed temperature sensing and leveling. These indexes are not manufacturer contact numbers. No source gives individual contact functions, mating-face orientation, or the fitted unit's exact revision.

The X1 Series MC/AP Cable Pack identifies two separate groups: an MC-to-AP power cable and an AP-to-MC communication cable. It describes power from motion control to application processor and data in both directions, but does not publish connector counts or contact assignments. The toolhead, touchscreen, camera, storage, mains, controller, processor, cooling and onboard-sensor cards are source context without added contact positions. Across the 13 source cards there are zero candidate routes and zero guesses. No external DB25 or stepper-driver/endstop pin map is established.
