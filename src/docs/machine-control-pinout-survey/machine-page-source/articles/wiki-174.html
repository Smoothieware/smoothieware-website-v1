Millport SmartLathez 1740 CNC lathe

Identity

Artisans Asylum identifies a Millport SmartLathez 1740, serial 622005, with a Centroid controller. The site also operates a separate Tormach 15L Slant-Pro family; this dossier covers the Millport only.

Wiki evidence

  • Artisans Asylum machine page: maker/model/serial, safety/training, CAM workflow and machine use.
  • Artisans Asylum CNC lathe workflow: startup, tool offsets, workholding and turning-operation notes specific to the SmartLathez 1740.
  • Artisans Asylum metal lathe safety/category: shared hazard guidance.

Operating information

The wiki describes loading G-code from a USB drive, checking way oil/coolant, powering and homing the machine, loading the program and reviewing its toolpath before setting tool offsets. It names Centroid control and Tri-Cool coolant. The wiki warns that the tailstock can trip the furthest Z limit and that a default parting feed can destroy tooling; use current training and verified setup values.

Visuals and pinout

The wiki has machine/safety images and a linked manual. No numbered controller or connector pinout was established from the wiki pages reviewed.
