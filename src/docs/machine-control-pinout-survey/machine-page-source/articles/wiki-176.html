Nichols Model 10-B Therm-O-Vac

Research status: wiki-sourced vacuum-former dossier; exact model absent from the existing repository search. Captured 2026-09-23.

Identity and configuration

The MakeICT FabLab inventory lists an operational Nichols model 10-B Therm-O-Vac from Industrial Arts Supply Co. Its machine page records a 14 × 20 inch frame, thermoplastic sheet thickness of approximately 0.5–1.5 mm, and a single three-way switch.

Wiki-described process and use

The wiki describes placing a mold over a perforated surface, clamping and heating plastic until it softens, then drawing it over the mold with vacuum. The resulting sheet follows the mold contour. The page gives approximate heating times by thickness: HIPS/PETG 15/30/45 seconds for 0.5/1/1.5 mm sheets; ABS 20/40/60 seconds. It warns that these values are approximate and says the machine cannot heat LDPE sufficiently for forming.

The page says the access policy had not yet been established at its last edit. Do not assume the machine is currently available or that this page is a sufficient safety/operating induction.

Connections and pinout

Only a single three-way operator switch is identified. The wiki does not label switch positions, terminals, supply voltage, heater circuit, vacuum pump wiring, or protective devices. No numbered pinout or schematic is published.

Visual evidence

The article embeds an image identified as the Nichols Model 10-B, plus a wiki-linked video of that machine in use. The image remains at the wiki because the page’s image reuse license was not verified. MakeICT Vacuum Former wiki page · FabLab inventory.

Evidence limits

Frame size and process notes are community-wiki claims. Heater temperature, vacuum level, electrical ratings, and verified material compatibility are not specified.
