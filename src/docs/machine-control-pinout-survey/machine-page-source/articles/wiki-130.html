Manufacturer-guide research · 2026-09-27

The official Creality Falcon2 Quick Guide V2.0 describes a 22 W variant; this does not establish the wattage or revision installed on the Falcon2 named by the wiki. The guide’s printed p. 05 lists 100–240 V AC input, a 24.0 V DC / 5.0 A adapter output, 400 × 415 mm engraving area, and 455 ± 5 nm wavelength.

The component overview (printed p. 06) names left/right Y-axis and front/rear X-axis assemblies, adapter board, 22 W laser module, control panel, emergency stop, security lock, TF slot, frame Type-C port, power input, and power button. The parts list (p. 07) names the power supply, air pump, USB-C cable, TF card/card reader, and USB-A-to-USB-C adapter. It also lists raiser stands, focus block, goggles, basswood, anti-static brush, storage box, hand tools, two lock keys, spare protective lens, screws, cleaning cloth, cable straps, metal protection plate, and tweezers. These supplied items are not assigned machine connector pins in the guide; the USB cables/adapters are accessories, not additional Falcon2 connector maps. The guide also describes AIR, FIRE, and LENS monitoring/indicators within the laser module (printed pp. 15–16); it publishes no separate external sensor contacts for them. The control-panel instructions name Frame, Start/Pause, and directional buttons, but give no panel electrical pinout. These are module/UI functions, not additional exposed machine pins. The guide does not provide machine-side motor, safety, panel, or harness cavity maps.

Assembly instructions (pp. 09–10) describe a pump cable to the machine’s left-side pump interface and a laser-module cable to its adapter board. Neither cable has a published contact count, pin assignment, voltage, polarity, connector family, or mating-face view. The guide warns against connecting or disconnecting the laser-module cable while powered (p. 04). Both interfaces remain OPEN.

The frame Type-C port supports host-computer operation with LaserGRBL/LightBurn (pp. 12–13); the module’s separate Type-C port is for firmware updates and work-log export (printed p. 16 / PDF p. 19). The guide gives neither port’s fitted-contact population, internal nets, or a SmoothieBox route. USB-IF’s 24-position receptacle map is eligible only as a generic form-only reference for each port. The TF slot remains position-unmapped: the SD Association distinguishes microSD assignments from its full-size SD schedule.

The two right-hand columns name the separate guide-identified ports; they do not assert fitted contacts or Falcon2 pin functions. All 48 port-position entries remain form-only and OPEN to SmoothieBox. This is a position-label inventory, not a mating-face drawing. A connector shell/shield is separate from the 24 contact designations; Falcon2 shell bonding is not documented and remains unmapped.
