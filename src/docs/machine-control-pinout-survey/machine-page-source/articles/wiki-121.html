BZT PFK 1607 PX

Research capture: 2026-09-23. Status: wiki-grounded draft; integration and whole-repository deduplication pending.

Identity, use and deduplication

BZT PFK 1607 PX gantry router at Happylab. Vienna and Berlin installations are discussed together; the installed controller revision is unknown. It is distinct from the atlas Avid, Onefinity, Shapeoko, OpenBuilds and Tormach machines; USBCNC software does not make it a duplicate of a generic parallel-port profile.

Wiki-supported machine facts

Wiki-listed travel: Vienna 1666 × 750 × 250 mm; Berlin Z 135 mm. Three stepper motors are listed at 4.2 A. Vienna spindle: 1.0 kW, 3500–24000 rpm; Berlin: Elte TMPE2 9/2 RR, 1.1 kW. The installations must remain separate when reusing ratings. Community machine page.

Control, setup and use

Wiki workflow uses USBCNC and VCarve. Secure stock on a sacrificial board with clamps and check jog travel for collisions before machining. The page reports 8000 mm/min rapid and 4000 mm/min working feed; these are source statements, not recommended cutting settings for arbitrary tools or materials. Community machine page.
