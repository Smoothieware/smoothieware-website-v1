Complete wiki-researched machine dossier

LulzBot TAZ 3

Research status: wiki-sourced model dossier; SoMakeIt lists one SMI TAZ 5 instance as this model. Captured 2026-09-23.

Wiki-recorded machine

SoMakeIt identifies the SMI TAZ 5 as a LulzBot TAZ 3. The wiki lists a 280 × 280 × 250 mm print volume, PEI bed, 0.5 mm nozzle (explicitly marked for confirmation), and PLA/ABS. The unit has no OctoPrint address listed; file transfer is via SD card. Slicing software is listed as LulzBot Cura 21.08, Slic3r, or PrusaSlicer. The page labels status “functional,” also marked for confirmation.

Use and limits

Use the documented SD-card path and treat the nozzle size and status as unverified wiki entries requiring a local induction/check. The page does not give a model-specific start-up or maintenance sequence.

Connections and visual

No electrical, connector, control-board, thermistor, heater, stepper, or endstop pinout is published in the cited article. Its page contains an instance caption but no inspected wiring diagram. SoMakeIt printer-model wiki page.

TAZ 3.0 manufacturer reference · not fitted-machine evidence

The 2013Q4 LulzBot TAZ 3.0 User Manual identifies the Y-axis stepper/endstop connector pair, heated-bed heater/thermistor pair, and separate four-position hot-end and extruder-stepper plugs. Its setup photographs and text give the connector counts and group functions, but no per-cavity pinout. The manual shows a USB receptacle and keyed DC power receptacle without contact schedules. The official LulzBot engineering index lists TAZ 3.0 and 3.1 as distinct designs. An OHAI page under a taz_3 URL is explicitly titled “TAZ 2.1 Final Assembly” and was excluded as TAZ 3 evidence.

Evidence limits

The model is a wiki identity, while its nozzle specification and operating status carry an explicit “confirmation needed” note. Do not count those fields as verified current condition.
