Rev 8 uses a straight-through DB25 logic cable; DB25 pin 14 is 50 Hz VFD PWM and pin 16 charge-pump input requires >10 kHz. The 12-position terminal carries supply and field I/O; DB9 carries motor power. G540 Rev 8 manual; G540 setup guide.

Documented evidence · unresolved wiring

Documented / version scope: Manufacturer Rev 8 manual publishes all three separate domains: DB25 logic pins 2/3 X step/dir, 4/5 Y, 6/7 Z, 8/9 A; 10–13 inputs, 14 VFD PWM, 15 fault, 16 charge pump, 1/17 outputs, 18–25 GND. DB9 motor-power output pins 1/5 current-set resistor, 2–4 NC, 6 B, 7 /B, 8 A, 9 /A. 12-position main terminal 1–4 inputs, 5–6 outputs, 7 VFD GND, 8 VFD OUT, 9 VFD +10 V, 10 disable/E-stop, 11 +18–50 VDC, 12 supply GND. Manufacturer initial setup guide adds charge-pump >10 kHz and DB9 motor hookup. Rev 8 says DB25 cable must be straight-through. The DB9 is not a step/dir logic connector. A V2-Core could replace upstream controller logic while retaining G540 only under a separately verified electrical/interface design; do not connect V2 logic directly to motor-phase contacts.
