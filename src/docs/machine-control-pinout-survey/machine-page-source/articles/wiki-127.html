Creality CR-10S

Research updated 2026-09-27. MakeICT lists this model as retired. The installed unit’s board revision, firmware, maintenance, and modifications are unknown.

Identity and configuration

The MakeICT wiki reports a 300 × 300 × 400 mm build volume, PLA/ABS use, 1.75 mm filament, 0.4 mm nozzle, nozzle maximum 250 °C, and bed maximum 100 °C. These are wiki entries, not measurements of the retired unit. The page’s stock image does not establish the physical unit’s condition.

Manufacturer manual and harness contacts

The Creality-branded CR-10 Series User Manual V5.1 explicitly names CR-10S in its model scope; the available copy is hosted by a third-party mirror rather than an official Creality domain. Page 4 (PDF page 9) shows four six-position/four-wire stepper groups (X/Y/Z/E) and three three-position/two-wire limit-switch groups (X/Y/Z), matched by yellow labels. The motor and switch plugs appear as white rectangular housings. A separate photograph labels circular control-box ports as aviation connectors; it does not identify the motor or switch plug series. The diagram lists 24 plus 9 nominal form slots. Its stable 1–6 and 1–3 indices are editorial only, not cavity numbers or orientation: the manual supplies no conductor/cavity mapping, winding-pair assignment, limit polarity, or NO/NC state.

Firmware/controller maps

The Klipper 2017 CR-10S configuration and Creality factory firmware repository assign MCU or board logical pins to motion, endstop, heater, sensor, fan, and display functions. These firmware references do not identify external harness cavities or the MakeICT unit’s fitted board. They are not external peripheral pinouts and are not wired to SmoothieBox in this diagram. Klipper’s example bed section sets `max_temp: 130`, while the manual lists a 100 °C bed maximum; this software threshold does not establish a higher machine rating.

Route status

All 33 nominal manual-counted form slots remain OPEN; the manual does not assign individual cavity functions or conductor identities, and no SmoothieBox route is established. SmoothieBox MOTOR CONTROL contacts are STEP/DIR/ENABLE logic. Do not treat them as motor winding outputs or connect them directly to the CR-10S stepper leads. No documented external driver stage or MakeICT retrofit was found. Heater, bed, thermistor, fan, LCD, filament-sensor, USB/SD, and mains are editorial unresolved function buckets, not eight manufacturer-documented connectors. No revision-matched physical contact map was found for them.
