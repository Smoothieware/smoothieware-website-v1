The numbered DB9/XLR rows are manufacturer contact IDs, not a left-to-right mating-face sketch. Identify the actual male or female view in the source artwork before using a physical connector. Motor-phase output is downstream of a drive; V2 Core STEP/DIR must never connect to these contacts.

Interface reading

Signal domains: Motion control or motor phases, limits/probe, spindle and safety where fitted. Controller family: Avid EX 14-pin.

What is still missing

Plasma-only cable omits Spindle OK contacts 1–2; servo harness is not the stepper DB9/XLR map. Confirm EX lid revision, option cable population and safety chain.

Documented evidence · unresolved wiring

Documented / version scope: Avid EX 24.1 stepper schematics, official page document version 2026Q2.1, CRP5210-01E interconnect. The missing motor phase table: DB9 NEMA23 pins 1/5 current-set resistor, 2–4 NC, 6 B+ yellow, 7 B− blue, 8 A+ red, 9 A− green; XLR NEMA34 1 A+ red, 2 A− green, 3 B+ yellow, 4 B− blue. These connectors are drive outputs, not step/dir. 14-pin spindle/plasma peripheral cable: 1/2 Spindle OK ground/signal, 3/4 torch ON, 5/6 divided voltage −/+, 7 FWD, 8 DCM, 9 analog control voltage, 10 analog common; 11–14 unused. Spindle and plasma cable populated variants differ. Unresolved: matching cable version, mating view, 0–10 V signal limits and machine safety.
