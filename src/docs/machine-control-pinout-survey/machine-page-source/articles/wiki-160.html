Identity, use and deduplication

Winti’s 2015 build based on Lasersaur 14.03, with a taller frame intended to accommodate a future Z arrangement. This is one Lasersaur machine/design entry, not a separate count for every build or controller. No Lasersaur entry appears in the frozen atlas laser families.

Wiki-supported machine facts

Wiki states 100 W CO₂ laser, 1220 × 550 mm working area, stock envelope up to 1400 × 700 mm and maximum feed 8000 mm/min. A taller frame does not prove a completed motorized Z axis. Controller-board revision is unknown. Community machine page.

2026-09-26 source update · original board versus Winti build

The Lasersaur Driveboard v14.03 wiring page assigns functions on twelve eight-position RJ45 cables for limits, motor windings, doors, laser, chiller, E-stop and optional E-valve. The X/Y motor cables terminate after the original drive stage; original door, chiller and E-stop interlocks form a hardware safety chain. The Winti machine page confirms local modifications and a 100 W laser, cooling, ventilation and E-stop, but supplies no revision-matched installed cable continuity or laser PSU electrical contract. The original 96 positions are REFERENCE ONLY / OPEN. No original-board source function is promoted to a fitted Winti wire or SmoothieBox route.

Winti wiki material identifies the local modified build; the Lasersaur project page supplies original Driveboard v14.03 contacts only. Source roles do not establish a fitted Winti harness or retrofit approval.
