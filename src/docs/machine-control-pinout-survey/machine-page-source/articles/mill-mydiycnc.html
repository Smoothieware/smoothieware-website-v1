Documented evidence · unresolved wiring

Documented: MyDIYCNC Comprehensive Plans and Manual eBook, author manual v1.4 ©2011. PDF p. 75, motor-terminal left-to-right wire colors blue, red, green, black; PDF p. 79 explicitly cautions that DB25 ribbon conductors are not sequential pin numbers and individual pins must be identified; appendices pp. 127–128 show master wiring/color code. Spindle module on PDF p. 49 has 12 V operating supply, 5 V logic and 3–24 V DC spindle output, option-specific. Unresolved: the connector mating-face orientation remains unverified; wire order is not enough to label motor A/B phases, and software parallel assignments do not determine ribbon conductor order.
