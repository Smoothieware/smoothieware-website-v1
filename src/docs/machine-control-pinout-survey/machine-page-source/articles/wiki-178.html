Source-researched reference dossier

OLSK Large Laser

Identity source: Appropedia lists Inmachines, a 1000 × 700 mm format, and a 75 W CO₂ laser. These are catalogue attributes, not proof of an individual machine’s installed configuration.

V1 source and revision boundary

The OLSK Large Laser V1 README identifies MKS-SBASE and grbl-LPC, while its linked firmware filename refers to grblHAL Teensy4. Separately, the OLSK Electronics repository names Laser Controller Shield V1.0 for Large Laser V1 and Small Laser V2. The V1 shield schematic is dated 2022-09-19 and depicts Teensy 4.1, ATtiny45, relay, CN1/CN2 motor-control headers, CN3 laser interface, two three-position STOP sensor headers and a 12 V power connector. It is a separate design reference: the sources do not prove that this shield is fitted to the unit in the Board2022 wiring sheet or resolve the MKS/Teensy conflict.

Contact inventory and wiring status

The visible SmoothieBox diagram lists 106 positions across 21 machine-side reference groups from the V1 machine wiring sheet and shield schematic. Printed driver, PSU, sensor, plug and shield positions are identified individually. Unnumbered or functionless marks are explicitly labelled as editorial drawing positions or unknown leads. All 106 are OPEN in this revision; it claims no fitted cable, electrical compatibility, DB25, or wiring approval. Mains, laser high-voltage, fire/enable and water-protection interfaces remain outside candidate routes.

Visual transcription correction

The fan-equipped nine-terminal V± supply is drawn separately from the far-right laser PSU; its output voltage and rating are unprinted. The laser PSU has the separate six-terminal control and three-terminal mains blocks. The chiller drawing labels only leads 1 and 3; no contact 2 is transcribed. The unsupported six-position-plug entry has been withdrawn.

The retained inventory contains 93 printed source-marked positions and 13 editorial lead/conductor references. The separate shield field connectors contribute 21 positions; its board-local ISP/test contacts are outside that subtotal. The remaining unnamed controller terminals and depicted component leads are not a reconciled physical contact map. CN1/CN2 are Teensy controller outputs, so no SmoothieBox STEP/DIR output is routed to them. All 106 positions remain OPEN with zero guesses.
