The RDC6445G/S V1.5 manual, PDF pages 20–24 describes DIR/PUL as common-anode open-collector outputs. Water protection and PSU permit wiring need the fitted PSU model and safety design; output labels alone do not validate that circuit.

Documented evidence · unresolved wiring

Documented / version scope: G V1.2 manual is G-only; separately cited G/S V1.5 manual, PDF pp. 20–24, gives CN1 general outputs, CN2 general inputs, CN3/CN4 limits, AXIS_X/Y/Z/U step/dir and CN5/CN6 laser power. From V1.5 p. 21: CN1 1 GND, 2 Out2/system error, 3 Out1/standby, 4 Status, 5 Wind/blower, 6 +24 V; outputs are optically isolated OC. CN2 p. 21–22: 1 GND, 2 FootSW, 3 DrProc door/protection, 4 Shot manual laser, 5 IN1. CN4 p. 22: 1 GND, 2 Y− limit, 3 Y+ limit, 4 X− limit, 5 X+ limit, 6 +5 V; CN3 is corresponding Z/U limit connector. Axis connectors p. 23: 1 DIR, 2 PUL, 3 +5 V; DIR/PUL are open collector, common-anode driver interface. CN5 p. 23–24: 1 GND, 2 L-ON1, 3 LPWM1, 4 WP1 water protection, 5 L-AN1 analog laser power; CN6 is second laser channel with analogous signals. Water protection is a safety input, not optional decoration; manufacturer says 24 V logic for WP. Variant-specific PSU and wiring remain unknown. The PDF text near CN2 pin 6 is flow/OCR ambiguous, so omit it until visual table inspection.
