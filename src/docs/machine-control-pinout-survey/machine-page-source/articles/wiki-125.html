Cloudray MP-60 LiteMarker Pro

Identity

Fabbulle's wiki names its fibre laser as an MP-60 LiteMarker Pro, 60 W, with a rotary axis. The page calls it “Cloudray” while separately naming JPT as the laser-module maker; retain those source roles rather than assuming the machine and module are the same product.

Wiki evidence

  • Fabbulle fibre laser page: names the MP-60 LiteMarker Pro, 60 W, rotary axis and 200 × 200 mm work area. It explains galvo mirrors, LightBurn workflow, material differences from CO₂ lasers, and the requirement to keep the enclosure closed and wear wavelength-appropriate protection.
  • The same page links wiki-held user and module manuals; exact connector pin assignments were not established in the captured material.

Operation and safety

The wiki says the enclosure must remain closed throughout marking and suitable eyewear is mandatory. It describes job setup in LightBurn and the galvo head's limited marking field. Observe local training and interlock requirements.

Manufacturer MP-series interface inventory

Cloudray’s LiteMarker Pro MP Series User Guide V3.3 (2024.08), pp. 14–18, names the PC-USB port, an optional 4-pin protective-cover cable, an optional 4-pin rotary cable, a foot-switch port, a 3-pin power inlet and an external ground pole. The family manual gives no contact functions or pin order. The Fabbulle page links an earlier V3.0 PDF whose CDN URL returned 404; V3.3 does not prove the installed Fabbulle revision.

  • Count-only placeholder positions: four each for optional protective-cover and rotary cables, three for the power inlet. They are not manufacturer pin numbers or a connector-face view.
  • USB and foot-switch pin counts are not stated. Their contact positions remain unspecified.
  • Power-input contacts, fitted supply voltage and grounding/bonding scheme remain unknown. Mains and protective earth are not SmoothieBox signal connections.
  • Fabbulle requires the enclosure to stay closed during marking; no interlock bypass is represented.

The manual’s internal laser, controller and rotary driver do not expose a fitted contact map. No verified installed controller, laser-module or machine-cable pinout appears in the reviewed sources, and no SmoothieBox route is established.

Uncertainty

The page's machine title uses the reseller brand, but it does not establish a complete OEM chassis model/revision or controller board revision. Confirm those from local labels before using any wiring diagram.
