Documented evidence · unresolved wiring

Documented: Homann Designs TurboTaig 2.2 manual, v2.2, PDF p. 6 DB25 J9/J10 alternatives and connector descriptions. The published connector table covers J1/J2/J29/J30 limits, J3 emergency, J4 enable, J5 selector; its DB25 pin 14 wording is internally conflicting and should be checked against manual circuit before use. This is an aftermarket/add-on TurboTaig, not the stock Taig DSLS control. Unresolved: installed J9 versus J10 route, contact view, revision and verified voltage/safety.

Supplemental contact map · 2026-09-27

The page 7 board view and text now add all three positions for each of J1, J2, J29 and J30: pin 1 GND/switch common, center pin 2 limit signal, and the opposite pin 3 +5 V sensor supply. J-number-to-axis mapping and installed fit remain open, so no endstop route is drawn. The diagram also adds the three J3 board-view positions and two J4 switch positions; only J3's center emergency signal and J4's switch-closure-to-ground behavior are sourced, with unresolved contacts left OPEN. These are not SmoothieBox-safe wiring instructions.

The two DB25 schedules remain alternatives: J9 for the listed Mach1/Master5/TurboCNC/EMC software family, or J10 for SuperCam User 1. Their 14 STEP/DIR matches to SmoothieBox motor-control terminals remain dotted guesses because board revision, electrical compatibility and cable continuity are unverified. No limit, emergency, enable, relay or common-ground path is added.
