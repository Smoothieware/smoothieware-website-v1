IndyMill CNC mill

Evidence depth: Appropedia catalogue identity only for the individual machine. Additional optional IndyShield V2 and GRBL references are explicitly revision-scoped; no particular build or controller is identified.

Wiki-supported identity and facts

The wiki catalogue lists 520 × 400 × 115 mm and a small Dremel-derived spindle, with 3D-printed parts. It calls the license unclear while noting source availability. These are registry-level claims, not a local build record.

Use, visuals, and electrical connections

The catalogue row does not identify a local operating procedure or installed wiring. The author-published IndyShield V2 Eagle archive contains 32 reference positions across three axis-drive blocks, three limit blocks, two spindle blocks, and RESET/HOLD/START/COOL/PROBE controls. Pin functions derive from its Eagle board nets cross-referenced with GRBL's Arduino Uno map. This is not proof that the individual IndyMill has an IndyShield, nor that an installed SmoothieBox should connect to that controller board.

The official parts list calls for four motors and four DM542 drivers, while the V2 schematic exposes labeled X/Y/Z blocks only. Community builds and author upgrades vary. The fourth driver, dual-Y arrangement, driver voltage/polarity, safety switch, and spindle interface remain unresolved; every contact is OPEN and no route or guess is proposed.
