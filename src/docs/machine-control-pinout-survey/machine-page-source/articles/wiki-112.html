Complete researched machine dossier

Anycubic Kobra

Identity and scope

The Artisans Asylum machine page records Make “anyCubic”, Model “Kobra”, and Serial “Unknown”, and links a user manual. The exact model name is therefore supported; the physical unit’s serial, installed controller/board, and build revision remain unknown. This record does not cover Kobra 2, Neo, Go, Plus, Max, or another variant.

Manual and source-labeled cable groups

Anycubic’s 2022-11-24 manual, version 0.0.5, shows four cable groups: 1 Print head, 2 X-axis motor, 3 Touchscreen, and 4 Z-axis motor. Those numerals label groups in the illustration, not contact or cavity positions. The manual gives no connector pin counts, mating-face orientation, pin functions, conductor order, polarity, or machine-to-controller pin schedule. Its overview names a Y motor, but the four-group cable illustration does not identify a Y-motor cable; no Y harness is inferred.

The manual says to match cable labels to ports and not connect or disconnect cables while powered. Its model specifications list 110/220 V AC, 50/60 Hz, 400 W, a 260 °C maximum extruder temperature, and a 110 °C maximum bed temperature; these are not connector ratings or retrofit assignments.

Other documented features and unresolved geometry

The product overview also labels a proximity switch, data-cable port, memory-card slot, power switch, and voltage selector. The manual says the proximity switch detects the metal bed for automatic leveling. These are contextual features only; the cited sources provide no contact schedule for them. The Artisans page gives a 200 × 200 mm stage, while the manufacturer manual gives a 220 × 220 × 250 mm build size. These are distinct source claims and are left unresolved.

The examined sources do not establish a DB25 connector on this physical unit. This is not a claim that the unit has none. No DB25 peripheral, controller connector, or SmoothieBox route is inferred.
