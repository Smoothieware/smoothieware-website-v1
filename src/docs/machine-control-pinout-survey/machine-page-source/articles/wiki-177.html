2026-09-26 original V1 design-source update

The OLSK Large CNC V1 schematic at pinned commit e7081c49 supplies 48 external-driver terminal marks and four tool-sensor wire labels. The 24 dotted routes illustrate a possible common-cathode pairing of each STEP/DIR/ENABLE output and circuit GND to PUL±/DIR±/ENA±; the PDF does not specify that topology or identify a fitted driver. Verify optocoupler input thresholds at 3.3 V, polarity, current, return/isolation and A-as-right-Y motion setup before selecting any route. Motor outputs and driver supplies remain OPEN. The schematic also does not establish direct SmoothieBox control of the tool sensor, limits, air-cooled spindle/mains circuit, E-stop/contactors or switches; the independent safety chain must not be replaced by controller GPIO. This is original V1 design evidence, not a surveyed installation.
