Documented evidence · unresolved wiring

Documented: Conflict. SainSmart cross-model installation table, page table rows PROVerXL 4030 V1 and V2: V1 green laser cable 12V PWM GND; V2 cable 12V GND PWM (SKU 101-63-PXL43V2-LSC). Earlier SainSmart cross-use guide, “Overview” and annotated controller-port photo: 4030 control board says 12V - GND - PWM; red arrows on the photo point left positive, middle negative/GND, right signal. The annotated photo shows three green receptacle contact openings at the controller enclosure exterior, with the word “Laser” below; it does not show the cable's wire-entry side or terminal-screw face. Unresolved / version scope: Earlier guide never identifies that photographed controller's PCB revision or says V1/V2. Later table describes a V1 cable without stating which face/direction orders its functions; its V1 cable thumbnail is too small to read contact labels or wire colors reliably. Looking at opposite mating faces reverses left/right while keeping the middle contact fixed, which cannot by itself explain the table/photo disagreement about whether the middle is PWM or GND. Hardware revision, different cable end/terminology, or guide error remain unverified explanations. The earlier guide instructs wiring the detachable terminal plug with a screwdriver and warns against trusting wire colors. Unresolved: treat the V1 physical contact order as disputed; inspect the installed board/plug label and verify continuity before assigning a sticker. Do not invent contacts 1–3 or transfer the V2 order to V1.

2026-09-26 V1 reference update: SainSmart’s V1.3 June 2021 manual names X/Y1/Y2/Z rear motor outputs, side limits/probe/laser groups, and Spindle +/− motor power. Its laser-rotary guide counts four X-axis green screw-terminal conductors but warns wire colors vary; these are former-box post-driver motor outputs, not STEP/DIR inputs. The V1 laser table names optional 12V/PWM/GND cable functions, while the board photo guide shows 12V/GND/PWM in its pictured view. Their physical cavity correspondence is unresolved. The nine counted source marks remain OPEN; other motor, limit and probe groups have no verified individual map, and no installed box revision, module or SmoothieBox route is established.
