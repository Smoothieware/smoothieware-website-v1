Manufacturer-guide research · Creality K1

Creality K1 User Manual V1.0 names the front USB flash-disk port, 4.3-inch touchscreen, rear power outlet, rear toggle switch, nozzle assembly, filament detection, printing platform, side fan, and back fan. It states the print interface as “USB Flash Disk/LAN Printing”. The guide reports 100–120 V AC or 200–240 V AC at 50/60 Hz and 350 W; those appliance ratings do not identify inlet cavity functions.

The manual contains no K1 mainboard revision, external harness table, individually numbered machine contact, or pin assignment. Its related K1-family replacement page is excluded from connector transcription because its tags cover K1, K1 Max, and K1C while the image path is K1 Max-specific. No DB25 is documented.
