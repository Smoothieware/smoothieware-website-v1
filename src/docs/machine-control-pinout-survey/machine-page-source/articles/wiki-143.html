Complete wiki-researched machine dossier

3D FabLight 3000 W sheet-metal laser

Identity

Artisans Asylum's wiki identifies a 3D FabLight sheet-metal laser, model recorded as “3,000 Watt,” serial 30450. That model field is a power designation in the wiki, not a conventional alphanumeric model number.

Wiki evidence

  • Artisans Asylum 3DFabLight page: machine identity, supported sheet materials/thicknesses, operation prerequisites and startup sequence.
  • The page says the machine cuts thin sheet metal using a laser and air pressure. It requires both AC plugs, shop air, extraction, a closed door, released E-stop and homing before jobs.
  • Its material restrictions explicitly exclude stainless steel, galvanized/painted/coated stock and all non-metals, with fume/fire warnings.

Operation and visuals

The wiki directs operators to its operation manual and FabCreator instructions. It describes the offline → initialized → machine-not-homed → homed state sequence. The wiki page includes machine/control imagery; it does not provide an electrical connector map in the reviewed text.

Pinout

The local wiki has no controller pinout. A 2021 FabLight 1500/3000/4500 operator manual names Utility Panel connections and documents the VACUUM/VACUUM POWER cord-end forms. The exact manual revision and its ports are not confirmed for serial 30450. Its two IEC reference groups show only the standard L, N and PE functions, without mating-face orientation; each remains OPEN to SmoothieBox.

Family-level Utility Panel reference

  • Main Power input plug, Ethernet, VACUUM outlet and VACUUM POWER inlet are named on the manual’s Utility Panel; the local serial’s connector revision remains unverified.
  • GAS IN and GAS EXHAUST are distinct 3/8-inch tube interfaces. The manual warns that pressurised input belongs only at GAS IN.
  • The manual identifies a rear exhaust-duct port, plus an I/O panel with MAINS and ON indicators, a momentary key switch, and an Emergency Stop. These are not SmoothieBox pin assignments.
  • The manual’s cord descriptions imply a C13 appliance outlet at VACUUM and a C14 appliance inlet at VACUUM POWER. Their source-scoped terminal labels are L (line), N (neutral), and PE (protective earth); physical pin order and installed fit remain unverified.
  • Every family-reference position is OPEN. No mains, network, pneumatic, interlock, or control route to SmoothieBox is proposed; no dotted guesses are drawn.

Scope

This is industrial sheet-metal laser equipment and a distinct machine class from the CO₂ gantry cutters elsewhere in this research set.
