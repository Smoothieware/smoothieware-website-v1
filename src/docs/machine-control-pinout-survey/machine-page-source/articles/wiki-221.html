The rendered ULS manual diagrams support 18 printed positions across optional-board connectors J2, J6, J5 and J8. J5 is explicitly “FUTURE USE, DO NOT USE”; J8 function is unassigned. The guide warns the automation outputs are for informational status and do not support third-party safety devices. Option fitment is unknown; all contacts remain OPEN. No DB25 is present in the source map.

Updated manufacturer contact scope

The manufacturer manual explicitly covers discontinued VLS6.60 and supplies readable pin labels for its optional Automation Interface: J2 (8 positions), J6 (4), J5 (4 reserved/do not use) and J8 (2 marks with unassigned functions). These are option-board family references, not evidence of this catalogue machine’s installed option or external harness.

The J2 inputs are programmable and local per-input actions are unknown. J6 is a mode-selectable programmable status output; ULS explicitly warns outputs do not support third-party safety devices and are not intended to drive external devices. No SmoothieBox paths are proposed. See the complete research dossier.

Wiki sources

Appropedia supplies catalogue identity; the ULS VLS Platform User Guide supplies optional-interface family contacts and explicitly includes discontinued VLS6.60. Local option fitment is unknown.
