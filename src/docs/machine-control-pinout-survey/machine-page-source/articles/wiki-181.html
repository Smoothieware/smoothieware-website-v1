Machine-control reference register · TinyG v8h J1-J8

Eight source-reference groups, 68 individually numbered schematic entries, all OPEN. Four motor symbols contribute 48 repeated phase entries; J1/J6/J7/J8 contribute 20 power, control and switch entries. These counts describe schematic marks, not 68 field-connector cavities. The separate generic OX interface remains unidentified.

J2-J5 preserve every printed out, alt and alt2 mark. The drawing joins three marks per phase; the four-terminal TinyG guide does not establish their physical terminal correspondence. Motor channels are not assigned to OX axes. Shared J7/J8 GND pins are not invented as separate switch-return pins.

Scope: J1-J8 machine-control connector symbols only. Host/service/debug/expansion and internal regulator/driver-access symbols J9-J20 are excluded; this is not a complete TinyG board pinout. The source is Board 131 v8h, dated 2014-06-20; the example reports TinyG V8 without a letter suffix. Connector mating views and the fitted OX revision remain unknown.

Source review · 2026-09-28

Appropedia retains the generic OX identity and 310 × 480 mm design attributes. The OpenBuilds OX/TinyG V8 resource describes a separate example with a different frame and equipment; it does not identify this catalogue unit or its harness. The example's V8 label does not establish v8h.

The four supplied Synthetos v8h Board 131 PDFs were visually inspected: Sheet 1 CPU Section, Sheet 2 Power Section, Sheet 3 Motors 1 & 2, Sheet 4 Motors 3 & 4, dated 2014-06-20. Every J1-J8 source-symbol entry appears individually in the register and current diagram. Physical pad population, field-connector numbering, mating faces, motor-axis bindings and fitted wiring remain unknown.

The official TinyG guide describes four motor phase terminals A1/A2/B1/B2, while the v8h drawing prints three repeated phase-label sets per motor symbol. The diagram preserves that distinction. The homing source specifies 3.3 V switch inputs and warns against 5 V; no shared return, control level or power feed is assigned to SmoothieBox by name matching.

OpenBuilds OX CNC

Evidence depth: CNC mill; gantry design. This dossier is grounded in the Appropedia wiki entry only; references from that page to vendor, GitHub, or other non-wiki pages were not used as evidence.

Wiki-supported identity and facts

The wiki catalogue gives 310 × 480 mm and identifies the design as an OpenBuilds machine. It notes SketchUp as a file format and says build instructions are available through the project community. This is an OX design entry, not a claim about every WorkBee build that derives from the OX.

Use, visuals, and electrical connections

The captured wiki catalogue row does not provide a machine-specific operating procedure, connector table, electrical pinout, or transcribable wiring diagram for this model. Those items are recorded as unknown, rather than inferred from the model name.

Separate OpenBuilds OX/TinyG V8 example

OpenBuilds also documents one specific OX build controlled by a TinyG V8. That example is a separate configuration, not evidence that the Appropedia catalogue unit has a TinyG controller or the example's harness. The TinyG v8h Board 131 schematic identifies controller-side terminal labels on J1, J6, J7 and J8; those labels describe the reference board and do not establish OX-side wiring, installed revision, connector mating view, or SmoothieBox routes. No DB25 is established by these sources.
