Blue Elephant ELE1325ATC-R · Bumblebee

Evidence scope: HSBNE identifies this sheet router as Blue Elephant ELE1325ATC-R, called Bumblebee, and links Syntec 6 documentation; the fitted controller model and revision are not identified. The local operational guide describes workflow and controls, not electrical terminals. Generic Syntec documents remain reference-only and are not transferred to this machine.

Machine identity and operating context

The HSBNE page describes an approximately 1300 × 2500 mm bed, ISO30 tooling and an eight-position automatic tool changer. It gives conflicting spindle entries (18,000 rpm versus 9 kW / 24,000 rpm), which remain unresolved. Its “X/Y/Z + A/Y2” notation and a rotary-looking fixture do not establish a fitted fourth-axis configuration.

The operational guide names X−, Y−, Z+ and 4+ homing indicators; E-stop controls on the controller and both gantry sides; ATM tool-length measurement; USB file import; and a 0.73 MPa pressure alarm. These identify functions and operator controls, not switch or controller terminals, connector counts, polarity, or safety-circuit topology. Its fourth-axis workflow does not identify installed axis wiring.

Controller references and limits

The linked Syntec 6 Series Operation Manual is a generic manual dated 2014-03-02, version 1.21. It does not establish the HSBNE machine's controller model/revision or cabinet terminal schedule. Blue Elephant's generic buyer guide describes Syntec 6MB capacity as 32 inputs and 32 outputs and gives an example four-axis ATC allocation of 8 outputs and 13 inputs. Those are logical-capacity/example figures, not physical contact counts or fitted assignments. The guide says its standard circuit drawing is available only through a separate technical-exchange group; that drawing is not present here.

The separate Mach3 USB manual covers another control architecture and is excluded from the P1P/Syntec assessment. No exact-machine source establishes a DB25, so this profile shows none. No source supports a machine pin map, route, or guess.
