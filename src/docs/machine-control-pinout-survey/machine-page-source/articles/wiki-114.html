Source-reviewed machine interface dossier

Bambu Lab P1S

Evidence scope: The exact-model Bambu Lab Quick Start Guide and two Bambu replacement-part listings support the facts below. These sources do not identify the fitted unit's board or cable revision, provide a mating-face view, or establish a SmoothieBox connection. The Appropedia catalogue entry supports model identity only.

Official P1S guide

The 2023 Quick Start Guide (document Y.BC.SM.A00201-01) labels the rear USB port, a four-pin Bambu Bus port and the mains power socket. Its component drawing also identifies the screen/LCD cable and Micro SD slot. The guide names a Dual-Core Cortex M4 motion controller and five closed-loop fan functions: Part Cooling, Hot End, Control Board, Chamber Temperature Regulator and Auxiliary Part Cooling. It does not provide contact functions or mating-face orientation for these interfaces.

The guide rates the rear USB output at 5 V / 1.5 A and the input at 100–240 VAC, 50/60 Hz, with maximum power of 1000 W at 220 V or 350 W at 110 V. These are source specifications, not a SmoothieBox wiring plan. The mains socket is context only. Fan names are functional context; connector counts, per-contact roles and harness mapping are not given.

OPEN contact inventory

The current source overlay lists 34 OPEN positions: 6 and 12 on the two ends of the P1 Series toolhead cable; 6 and 6 on the ends of the heatbed signal cable; and 4 at the rear Bambu Bus port. Each displayed number is an editorial inventory index, not a source-stamped cavity number. The listings do not assign individual signals or identify installed cable revision and end orientation. All positions remain OPEN, with zero proposed routes and zero guesses.

The USB port, mains socket, screen/LCD cable and Micro SD slot remain named peripherals without contact schedules in the cited evidence. This profile does not establish stepper-driver terminals, endstop wiring or an external DB25 connector. Do not transfer generic controller or printer pinouts to the P1S.
