The Mesa 7I96S manual, PDF pages 10–14 gives TB3 isolated 5–36 V inputs, four high-current DC outputs and two isolated AC/DC outputs. These controller-board contacts are not an installed machine harness; verify board revision and loads.

Documented evidence · unresolved wiring

Documented / version scope: Manufacturer 7I96S manual, PDF pp. 10–14: P4 power pin 1 +5 V (square pad), 2 GND. TB1 is differential step/dir outputs 0–3; +5VP terminals are PTC protected. TB2: pins 1 GND, 2 STEP4−, 3 STEP4+, 4 DIR4−, 5 DIR4+, 6 +5VP, 7/8 ENCA+/−, 9 GND, 10/11 ENCB+/−, 12 +5VP, 13/14 IDX+/−, 15 GND, 16/17 RX+/−, 18/19 TX+/−, 20 +5VP, 21 NC, 22 SPINDLE−, 23 SPINDLE OUT, 24 SPINDLE+. TB3: pins 1–11 INPUT0–INPUT10, 12 INPUT COMMON, 13/14 OUT0−/+, 15/16 OUT1−/+, 17/18 OUT2−/+, 19/20 OUT3−/+, 21/22 OUT4A/B, 23/24 OUT5A/B. Manual says TB3 has 11 isolated 5–36 V inputs, 4 high-current DC outputs and 2 high-isolation AC/DC outputs. This is a controller reference, not a machine-specific harness; check manual revision against hardware.
