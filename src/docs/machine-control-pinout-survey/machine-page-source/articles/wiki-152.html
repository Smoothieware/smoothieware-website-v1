RepRap Huxley (community design)

Variant boundary

This entry describes the general RepRap Huxley community design. The Huxley page links separate RepRapPro, TechZone and eMaker kit instructions; those kit implementations are not evidence for this profile. RIS 1 compliance permits alternative electronics and does not identify which controller, if any, is fitted to a particular Huxley.

Source-supported peripherals and contacts
  • The Huxley design lists three NEMA 14 steppers and one NEMA 17 extruder stepper; the extruder is Bowden. It does not map the three smaller motors to axes or specify motor terminals, connectors, winding pairs, or cable order.
  • RIS 1 is a controller feature set: at least four stepper motors, three RIS 2 endstops, one thermistor sensor, and one heater of at least 60 W. It explicitly allows single-board or distributed electronics and does not prescribe system voltages.
  • RIS 2 defines a conditional 3-pin endstop header reference at 2.54 mm pitch, Molex KK100-compatible, with the standard diagram order Vs | GND | Vcc. It assigns no numeric pin numbers. The standard documents separate 3.3 V and 5 V electrical variants, and specifically says Sanguinololu 1.3a does not match this pinout; neither option is selected for this Huxley profile. These labels are standards references only; Huxley-specific fit, orientation, axis mapping and continuity are not established.
  • No machine-side DB25, fitted SmoothieBox connector map, endstop harness or machine-specific electrical pinout was identified. All SmoothieBox routes remain OPEN and no guess is drawn.
