Documented evidence · unresolved wiring

Documented: TwoTrees TS2-10W board page embeds image, manufacturer image shows: component-top view, power input left/bottom, X/Y/Z motor outputs, 12/24 V output group and TTL output at top right, X/Y endstop group, Z-axis/gyroscope, flame, I²C. Board silk says MKS DLC32 V2.0. Unresolved / version scope: Drawing's filename 2.1.png is not board revision; board silk V2.0. Group labels lack contact pins. Do not infer direct STEP/DIR or 24 V laser power from a 12/24 V group label alone.

Current cable-count update: The manufacturer-authored TS2 10 W manual mirror, PDF page 12 / printed page 19, counts eight factory cables and 23 positions. It lists one Y cable despite the pictured board’s Y1/Y2 output options, and names X/Z limits rather than an installed Y-limit cable. Position ordinals in the current SVG are inventory labels only; the manual does not publish cavity numbers, cable colours, conductor roles or module-side pin order. The Makerbase MKS DLC32 V2.0_001 schematic describes a reference board, not installed harness continuity. Its motor sockets are outputs after plug-in drivers, not STEP/DIR inputs; its input-fed +12V net is not proof of a regulated laser supply. No physical SmoothieBox wiring is selected.
