Shop Sabre Sidekick 12

Research status: Quelab wiki documents a donated and installed Shop Sabre CNC router. Captured 2026-09-23.

Identity and deployment

Quelab names the machine Shop Sabre Sidekick 12. Its wiki says it was donated by Ideum Inc. in 2021, weighed over 3000 lb, and was moved and installed by a professional rigging crew. A member added warning lights and additional E-stops and brought the machine to a usable state. The wiki calls it a red-level tool and requires training/certification.

Wiki-recorded use

The page says Vectric VCarvePro Makerspace Edition is used to prepare files, which can be created on a personal computer and brought to the machine. It does not document a complete local setup, postprocessor, tool-change, homing, or machining procedure.

Connections and pinout

The wiki confirms additional emergency-stop buttons and warning lights were installed, but provides no circuit diagram, contact numbers, voltage, interlock, spindle, axis-drive, or pendant wiring. The E-stop count and physical locations need current on-site verification.

Visual evidence and source

The Quelab page contains installation/setup photographs, not an electrical schematic in the inspected text. Quelab Shop Sabre CNC wiki page.

Evidence limits
