Documented evidence · unresolved wiring

Documented / version scope: Revision conflict requiring explicit source labeling, not a simple mislabel: The Avid 2020Q3.1 PDF has 20.1 in its filename but its cover and every page header say “17.4 Electronics Revision” (PDF pp. 1, 17–18). Its CRP850 table on PDF p. 18 has 42 terminals: 33/34 spindle relay FWD/DCM, 35/36 PWM/5V ground, 37/38 0–10V/analog common, 39/40 12V input/ground, 41/42 fault/ground. Conversely Avid's current 17.4 HTML schematic, CRP850 Phoenix terminal table, has 36 terminals: 27/28 FWD/DCM, 29/30 PWM/5V ground, 31/32 0–10V/12V ground, 33/34 12V input/ground, 35/36 fault/ground. Both Avid documents claim the same 17.4 electronics revision and CRP850-00E, yet their numbering and extra terminals materially disagree. Avid manual revision index places 17.4 purchases in Sep2017–Dec2019 and 20.1 in Jan2020–Mar2021; this makes the filename suspicious but does not override the PDF's explicit 17.4 headers. The official 20.1 HTML schematic independently shows the same 42-terminal table, including 33/34 FWD/DCM and 37/38 analog, as the disputed PDF. This strongly suggests stale 17.4 page headers in the PDF, but that remains an inference; the filename plus table agreement do not justify silently overriding its printed revision. The cited PDF must be read alongside the conflicting 17.4 HTML and matching 20.1 HTML, then require physical board/photo/part revision or manufacturer clarification before any 17.4 terminal label. Both documents' peripheral 14-pin table uses 7 FWD, 8 DCM, 9 analog, 10 analog common; the PDF prose says 8/9 for analog despite table and board text saying 9/10. 17.4 HTML also publishes DB9 NEMA23 motor outputs (1/5 current-set resistor, 2–4 NC, 6 B+ yellow, 7 B− blue, 8 A+ red, 9 A− green) and XLR NEMA34 motor outputs (1 A+ red, 2 A− green, 3 B+ yellow, 4 B− blue). Motor contacts are phase outputs, not step/dir. Unresolved: identify installed CRP850 physical layout and exact dated manual; source discrepancy is unresolved, not corrected by choosing either map.
