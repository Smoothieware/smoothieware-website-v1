Creality Ender 3 S1 3D printer

Identity

Artisans Asylum records the maker as Creality and model as Ender3S1. Its shop category lists two units; operational status varies by the current shop inventory.

Machine details

The machine page describes a 200 × 200 mm Y-moving bed using rollers on an extruded rail and belt drive; Z uses two screw jacks and two motors; X uses belt-driven rollers. The 1.75 mm extruder has a proximity sensor. The category says nozzle temperature can reach 200–300 °C and the bed about 100 °C, and requires users to stay clear of moving parts.

Manufacturer manual interface inventory

Creality’s 2023 manual §4.1 labels the mainboard-facing heated-bed thermistor, heated bed, DC 24 V input, fuse, Z-motor interface, filament-sensor interface, Z-limit interface, mainboard fan, touchscreen, knob display, memory-card slot, and Type-C port. Section 4.2 names a 24P FFC and the nozzle heater, thermistor, extrusion motor, two fans, and auto-leveling interface. Section 4.3 labels filament-sensor, +24 V, −24 V, PWM, and Z-motor interfaces on the Z-axis adapter. The manual supplies no contact-by-contact assignments, orientation, polarity, or FFC-to-device map; all positions are OPEN. The Artisans Asylum unit’s fitted board and harness revisions are unverified.
