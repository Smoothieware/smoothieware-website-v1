2026-09-26 first-generation source update: The 3018-PROVer User Manual v1.1 (April 2020), first-generation assembly guide and official offline-port guide support 52 selected source-reference positions across 16 cards. The manual identifies the GC3D3 board. Its wiring pages document six separate X±/Y±/Z± limit cables despite a duplicated Z-LIM− parts-list entry; motor sockets are former-board winding outputs, not STEP/DIR inputs. The spindle red M+/black M− leads are motor power. The EStop button replaces a shorting jumper, but the sources do not establish contact polarity or a safety-rated stop. The 10-position offline header belongs to the former controller. Optional -LAS+ supply, -PWM+ and GND/0–5 V spindle-controller outputs are separate and do not prove fitted laser or controller hardware. No connector cavity order, installed cable continuity, qualified power stage or SmoothieBox route follows from these references.
