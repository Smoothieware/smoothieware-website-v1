Trotec manual 8070 names these family interfaces but provides no physical pin schedule for the I/O, service, LAN or optional accessory connectors. The two USB entries are functional references, not cavity pinouts. Exhaust interfaces are mechanical hose connections. All electrically relevant routes remain OPEN; no guess is drawn and DB25 is not identified; no connector form is assumed. Main power and internal high-voltage/laser circuitry are excluded.

Updated source scope

The Happylab wiki documents a RUBY-operated Speedy 400 and describes CO₂ use. The retained machine photo shows a “flexx” marking, which does not establish the fitted laser source. Trotec Manual 8070 (2022) is used only for Speedy 400 family interface names; it is not confirmation of this installation’s model revision or optional accessories.

The manufacturer labels USB charging/data ports, rear LAN, Wi-Fi dongle, I/O and service interfaces, a rotary connector and exhaust duct connections. It supplies no I/O or service connector pinout. The main electrical and safety connections remain outside this SmoothieBox contact study. See the complete research dossier.
