3DMakerWorld Artifex

Identity

Artifex is a 3DMakerWorld open-source desktop FFF printer design based partly on MendelMax 1.5/2.0. It remains a distinct design with its own frame, electrical and mechanical specification.

Wiki evidence

  • RepRap Artifex, revision 114817: model description, design-reported electrical/mechanical specifications, and links to named documents.
  • Artifex User Manual (January 2014 mirror): named manufacturer document; third-party hosted copy. Artifex design repository: published mechanical files and README; no electrical schematic or harness schedule. Current 3DMakerWorld Support: Artifex 2 material kept separate from this legacy profile.

Specifications and operation

The versioned Artifex design record names RAMBo electronics, a MakerGear hot end with a 0.35 mm nozzle, a 24 V / 200 W heated bed, three 24 V cooling fans (filament drive, print surface, electronics), 110/220 VAC power requirements, X/Y linear-rail and GT2-belt motion, and Z precision shafts with ACME leadscrews. These are design facts, not external connector assignments or a fitted-unit inspection.

Pinout and visuals

No per-contact harness map was found in the accessible, captured excerpts and inspected sources. RAMBo board terminal and firmware labels do not identify the Artifex external harness. No DB25 is evidenced, and the 110/220 VAC requirement is context only, not a SmoothieBox route. The named hot end, bed, fans, and motion references are not mapped to SmoothieBox contacts.

Limits

The specifications apply to the Artifex wiki design record. Verify the actual RAMBo revision, hotend and bed before wiring.

Source scope and limits are documented in the research dossier. Artifex 2 material is not substituted for the legacy design.
