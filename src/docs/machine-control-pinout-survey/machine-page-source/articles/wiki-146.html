Machine and connector evidence

Formlabs Form 2 SLA printer

Identity and operation

RaumZeitLabor's wiki identifies the installed fab-lab printer as a Formlabs Form 2 and gives its network-print workflow. That local wiki is identity and operating-context evidence, not a hardware pinout.

Formlabs hardware and external interfaces
  • The Form 2 product glossary identifies a 100–240 V internal power supply that converts line power to +24 V DC; three motherboard motor-driver chips drive the build-platform, wiper and tank-carrier steppers; a motherboard-to-front-display ribbon; built-in Wi-Fi; and rear-accessible USB and Ethernet ports.
  • Formlabs' connection instructions identify the Form 2 rear Ethernet connector as RJ-45 and list 10BASE-T/100BASE-TX. The diagram enumerates eight connector-reference positions without assigning Form 2 pin functions.
  • The same article describes the supplied USB cable as the wired computer connection but does not identify the Form 2 port subtype or contact schedule. Its USB-C statement applies to Form 4/Form 4L and is not transferred to Form 2.
  • Form 2 technical specifications list 100–240 V AC, 1.5 A, 50/60 Hz and 65 W. Connector type, terminal count and protective-earth mapping are not established by these reviewed sources.
  • Formlabs describes a 405 nm, 250 mW maximum violet diode laser with Class 1 classification. No external laser power supply or electrical contacts are documented for this printer.
Pinout and route limits

All eight RJ-45 positions, where individually shown, remain OPEN to SmoothieBox. USB, mains, motor, display-ribbon, Wi-Fi and laser contact schedules are unknown or internal. No external step/direction/enable input is documented for the Formlabs motherboard drivers. No SmoothieBox wire, guessed route or DB25 connector is established.

Other limits

Resin handling and post-processing instructions in the local wiki are incomplete. This wiring diagram does not serve as a service manual or authorize opening the printer or modifying mains, laser or internal motor circuits.
