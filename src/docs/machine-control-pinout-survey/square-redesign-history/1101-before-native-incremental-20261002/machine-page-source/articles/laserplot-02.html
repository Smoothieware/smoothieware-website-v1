Current installation sequence
  1. Match the actual machine and controller revisions, trace the unpowered harness and verify all signal/reference, power and independent safety boundaries before energising. Follow the current connection IDs rather than the superseded architecture prose.
  2. X min limit · signal and ground separately: Trace cable with power isolated; confirm dry contact and NO/NC state. Leave selector pin 1 unused for passive switches; powered sensors require a matched interface.
  3. Y min limit · signal and ground separately: Trace cable with power isolated; confirm dry contact and NO/NC state. Leave selector pin 1 unused for passive switches; powered sensors require a matched interface.
  4. K40 laser power supply · control and reference: G/IN/5V are source labels. Confirm the fitted PSU model, input threshold and compatible level interface; the Mini Gerbil installation is not evidence of direct Prime 3.3 V compatibility. Laser firing enable is a separate circuit, not supplied by intensity PWM.
  5. Alternative L/LO-labelled PSU firing boundary · mutually exclusive with TL variant: Identify active level, firing terminal and water/door/key/E-stop contacts from the fitted PSU manual. Retain the independent hardware inhibition chain; do not bridge an interlock. PSU 5V is an output/reference, not a Prime PWM pin. Choose the installed PSU variant only: the L/LO and TH/TL reference labels are alternatives, not two firing connections to install together.
  6. Prime selected · X onboard driver output: Selected replacement: integrated driver outputs directly to motor winding pairs. Verify selected Prime revision, current settings, supply rating and winding pairs.
  7. Prime selected · Y onboard driver output: Selected replacement: integrated driver outputs directly to motor winding pairs. Verify selected Prime revision, current settings, supply rating and winding pairs.
  8. Prime selected · separate logic and motor supplies: This is the alternative integrated-driver architecture. The same PCB-derived contact labels occur on P121/P12, but driver type, rating and current configuration differ. Use one verified power-entry arrangement; J10 parallels VMOT and is left unused here. Board ground is shared internally; route motor current directly to power distribution, not through logic wiring. Determine actual allowed voltage/current before choosing a supply; no machine-source adapter automatically qualifies.
  9. Laser protection and firing inputs · match fitted PSU variant: Cloudray MYJG-40 NW is a reference variant only, not proof of installed PSU. Its manual distinguishes TH/TL firing, WP water/fan protection, G reference and IN intensity. Follow matching manufacturer isolation and active-level requirements; do not bridge WP or remove water/door/E-stop checks. Existing L/LO labels elsewhere describe other possible PSU variants.

Replace the Mini Gerbil motion power outputs with two documented drivers, retaining X/Y motors after winding identification. Each motor circuit and switch return is now drawn separately. PSU intensity and firing are distinct interfaces; fitted PSU identification is required to finish those two connections without transferring an MG3 pin map onto MG2.

Which Smoothie target fits this machine?
  • SmoothieBox: Use a Core-based box for the drawn external-driver option, or a Prime-based box for the rated winding-output option. Trace the actual carrier harness; exterior connector names do not establish board pin numbers.
  • V2 Core: Main drawing uses Core P1 logic and new Pololu 2133 drivers. Use only when measured motor current and supply fit the carrier; otherwise select a suitably rated driver and redraw its exact terminals.
  • V2 Prime: Alternative: onboard winding outputs J5/J6/J7/J8, each pin 1 B2, 2 B1, 3 A2, 4 A1. Assign the required motors in firmware; check fitted P11/P12 current, voltage and thermal limits before replacing external drivers.
Retain or add
  • A separate documented driver per motor, branch fuses and enclosed terminal distribution; never connect Core STEP/DIR pins to motor windings.
  • Identify each winding pair by continuity with power isolated. Label the newly terminated leads A1/A2/B1/B2; do not infer original cavity numbering or wire colours.
  • Core logic power: regulated 5 V to J42.1, return to J42.2, with exact board revision and supply-path checks. Motor power uses its own fused distribution.
Conversion and commissioning
  1. Photograph nameplates, connector orientation and existing safety wiring before disconnecting anything. Match the fitted board revision to the linked hardware source.
  2. Disconnect the old motor-controller outputs before connecting the new drivers. Identify each coil pair and trace every endstop signal and return.
  3. Set driver current from the motor rating, verify supplies and pull-ups, and configure channels and enable polarity. Initial MODE pins open means full step; update firmware steps/mm when selecting microsteps.
  4. With process power disconnected, verify reset keeps drives disabled, test one motor at a time, then check endstop status, homing direction and travel. Enable the process only after its separate interface and hardware safety chain are proven.

Pin-by-pin schedule and commissioning notes
  • Mini Gerbil Type 2: J1 PWM is the source-described PWM connector; J3 low-voltage harness; J4 X motor only for ribbon harness; J6 Y motor outputs; J7 X motor output for non-ribbon harness; J5 limits. These are Mini Gerbil contacts, not Smoothie terminals.
  • K40 laser PSU control terminal marks: G, IN (middle), 5V. Polarity, logic threshold, isolation, reference, active level and exact PSU revision are not established by the installation guide; keep each mark separate and do not infer wire order from a mating view.
  • X/Y motor connector pin numbers, winding pairs, individual limit switch contacts, water/door interlock contacts and E-stop contact schedule are not source-mapped; leave each physical position UNMAPPED until exact board/manual revision and continuity are known.
  • Core external-driver reference: P1 J5 pin 16 /STEP_A, pin 18 /DIR_A; pin 22 /STEP_B, pin 24 /DIR_B; pin 28 /STEP_C, pin 30 /DIR_C; pin 34 /STEP_D, pin 36 /DIR_D; pin 13 /MOT_EN. These are board-source logical nets; do not assume the carrier/StepXternal harness, receiver polarity, enable behavior, or machine axis mapping from the net names. The machine channel-to-axis choice remains an explicit configuration decision and is shown dotted where proposed.
  • Core P1 J5 signal pin assignments for this proposed driver option are listed per conductor in the main diagram; the full carrier and machine-side contact inventories remain in the retained contact reference. The A–D channel-to-machine-axis pairings shown as proposals must be set in configuration and verified at the exact machine revision.
  • Additional MYJG-40 NW reference only, not established as fitted: IN accepts 0–5 V analog or PWM; cited manual requires PWM at least 20 kHz and peak at most 5 V. Low-level firing requires at most 0.3 V. Its P3 5V row incorrectly says output 24V, and later troubleshooting conflicts with the terminal/function tables. Do not transfer this inconsistent reference onto an unidentified PSU or treat it as a verified harness.
