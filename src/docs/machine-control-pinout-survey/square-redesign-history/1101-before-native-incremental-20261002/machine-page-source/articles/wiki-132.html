  7. Recycler · retain motor power stage and feedback on ESCON: Maxon J3 Hall schedule: 1 Hall1, 2 Hall2, 3 Hall3, 4 +5 V Hall supply (≤30 mA), 5 GND. J3 motor/Hall wiring is source-specific but the CubeFactory harness is unreported; OPEN breaks prevent fabricated phase/color assignments. ESCON 70/10 current rating must be reconciled with project claim of 10 A normal / 40 A maximum before use.
  8. Recycler heater and original controller · do not splice yet: Project text says Inkbird D1S-2R-24, but the visible photograph only shows INKBIRD/ITC front branding. Identify rear label, sensor, supply, output rating and the 24 V supply branch; then add exact contacts.
  9. Prime selected · separate logic and motor supplies: This is the alternative integrated-driver architecture. The same PCB-derived contact labels occur on P121/P12, but driver type, rating and current configuration differ. Use one verified power-entry arrangement; J10 parallels VMOT and is left unused here. Board ground is shared internally; route motor current directly to power distribution, not through logic wiring. Determine actual allowed voltage/current before choosing a supply; no machine-source adapter automatically qualifies.
  10. Printer home inputs · X, Y and Z switch pairs: Prusa/Hephestos family reference does not establish the fitted CubeFactory harness. This proposal uses newly identified passive switch leads; confirm each NO/NC state and firmware axis assignment with power isolated. Pin1 supply selectors stay unused. Powered sensors need an electrically matched interface, not this passive-contact circuit. Optional max inputs J25/J27/J29 remain unconnected unless actual additional switches are fitted and mapped.
  11. Prime P12 · Hotend A · complete external power and load circuit: Board-side evidence: exact P12 schematic/PCB at commit7dc4f9a identifies J17 Hotend A, J18 Hotend B, J19 Fan1, J20 Fan2. J17.1 receives shared Q25 cutoff-controlled VFET; J17.2 is Q19 switched return. These are factory internal circuits, not user soldering instructions. PE0 / HOTEND_A_FET and PF14 / OUTPUTENABLE come from the exact P12 source; a general documentation PJ6 label conflicts and must not override this revision. Heater/sensor fitment and connector-safe current are unknown: measure, qualify firmware and prove thermal shutdown before connecting heat. No guaranteed 5A terminal rating is asserted.
  12. Prime P12 · individual fan outputs and spare Hotend B: J19/J20 pin1 share the same VFET output from factory Q25; pin2 returns switch through Q21/Q22. No second power supply is connected to the fan outputs. Leave both J18 contacts unconnected in this one-hotend option. Actual hotend heatsink cooling must be maintained; do not assume an original fan is VFET-voltage rated.
  13. Printer hotend temperature · feedback required before any heat: BQ reference names temperature sensing, but fitted sensor type/lead pairs are not identified. Prime J21.1/.2 supports the board temperature input and return; establish NTC/PT100/thermocouple identity and configure a compatible sensor circuit and firmware. Heater power must remain disabled until feedback, fault detection and independent cutoff are proved. The recycler Inkbird heater remains an independent controller system.

Earlier alternative retrofit architecture and circuit details

Owner retrofit guide · CubeFactory 2 printer + recycler

The printer and recycler are separate systems. Prime provides four integrated stepper stages, so a measured printer motor winding pair can connect directly to the matching Prime phase outputs when the motor current and supply ratings fit. Core instead needs four selected external stepper drivers. The recycler BLDC phases and Hall feedback remain on the ESCON. Every dashed route is a proposed axis or software mapping, not a fitted-harness assertion.

Which Smoothie target fits this machine?
  • SmoothieBox: Use a SmoothieBox carrying V2 Prime for the four printer steppers only after matching rated motor current, motor supply, heater and sensor types. The box/carrier itself is not the motion controller.
  • V2 Core: V2 Core has no integrated stepper power stages; add four selected external stepper drivers and measure each motor winding/current. Core J5 A–D STEP/DIR are logical signals; slash-prefixed net names do not mean active-low.
  • V2 Prime: Preferred printer candidate because four integrated channels match the design-family X/Y/Z/E arrangement. Exact Prime connector J5/J6/J7/J8 phases are drawn; CubeFactory harness/polarity/current remains unverified. Keep recycler on ESCON. Thermal/fan proposal below is specifically the researched Prime P12 2590 variant, not a transferable P11 thermal pinout. Recycler sleeve heater remains on its independently controlled original system.
Retain or add
  • Retain Maxon ESCON 70/10 for recycler BLDC; verify current capability and exact model/configuration first.
  • Retain identified 24 V DC supply only if its measured/nameplate rating fits the ESCON and load.
  • Prime option: four motor phase outputs J5–J8; no external driver required if rating matches. Core option: four rated external drivers; exact driver model not documented.
  • Keep the separate heater controller until its rear label, output and sensor terminals are identified.
  • Conditional Prime P12 heater/fan stages: use J12 XT30 VFET entry, J17 Hotend A, J19/J20 fans only with measured matching loads, branch fuse and independent thermal cutoff; temperature feedback/firmware is a blocking qualification gate.
Conversion and commissioning
  1. Photograph and transcribe fitted controller, ESCON, motor, supply, printer board and heater-controller labels.
  2. Isolate power. Check ESCON current limit and motor rating against 10 A normal / 40 A maximum project statements; do not assume the 70/10 model can sustain 40 A.
  3. For Prime, continuity-test each printer motor to identify A/B winding pairs and verify phase current and supply range; connect only to J5/J6/J7/J8 after rating check. For Core, select matching external drivers and preserve the separate driver power stage.
  4. Verify ESCON DigIN1 PWM configuration, frequency, setpoint scaling, DigIN2 run function, reference and stop behavior before signal connection.
  5. Leave heater, sensors and limit switches OPEN until exact fitted device and terminal function are identified. Prove physical E-stop and guards independently of Smoothie.

This documents that firmware configuration, not the sensor or harness fitted to the CubeFactory2 unit. Do not infer table 1, a Hephestos 2 sensor, or a PT100 from the model name. Before direct wiring to Prime, identify the actual two-lead NTC, match its temperature-resistance curve, and verify the Prime input network and pull-up. Until those checks are complete, the machine interface remains a conditional proposal.
