# Mini Gerbil 2 K40 retrofit: source-scoped contacts

## Manufacturer evidence and scope

- [Current Type 1 PSU installation guide](https://awesome.tech/installing-mg-type1/) identifies Mini Gerbil J3 as the transferred low-voltage harness, J6 as the Y motor, J7 as the X motor **only for a non-ribbon K40**, J5 as the corresponding non-ribbon limit-switch plug, and J1 PWM as the lead to the laser supply's IN terminal. A ribbon build instead takes its X motor and limit switches through J4. The guide warns that wire colours in its photos are illustrative.
- [Current Type 2 PSU installation guide](https://awesome.tech/installing-mini-gerbil-on-the-k40-type2/) illustrates the non-ribbon variant. Its diagram labels J6 `Y-axis / Rotating tool`, J7 `Alternative X-axis to ribbon`, and four outputs per motor socket, left to right in that drawing: `A+`, `A-`, `B+`, `B-`. Its separate [J5 orientation image](https://awesome.tech/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/Install-non-ribbon-limit-switch-with-arrow.jpg) confirms `Gnd`, `Y`, `X`, `Gnd`, `+5Vdc` toward the ribbon connector, with the example harness using two common-ground wires in one cavity. The main diagram labels the Type 2 supply's three low-voltage terminals `G`, `IN`, `5V`; `IN` is the middle terminal and receives the single J1 PWM output lead after the existing control lead is disconnected and insulated. The images are illustrative controller drawings, not proof that every installed board or motor cable shares their contact view.
- The [older Mini Gerbil 2 installation page](https://awesome.tech/installing-mini-gerbil/) explicitly labels its photographed board **V1.1A**. The J3 power photo at `https://awesome.tech/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/MGpowerterminals.png` shows `LO`, `5V`, `Gnd`, `24V` left to right *in that photo*. Its J5 photo at `https://awesome.tech/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/MGLimitswitches.png` shows `G`, `Y`, `X`, `G`, `5V` left to right as viewed from the board edge toward the ribbon socket. This older page says later 2.0 boards swap the ribbon and limit-socket locations. Therefore do not transfer the V1.1A physical socket location to 2.0.

The atlas has two distinct profiles: `laserplot-01` is a V1.1A retrofit without a specified PSU type; `laserplot-02` is a Type 2 PSU retrofit without a confirmed board revision. These are manufacturer reference pinouts, not an observation of a particular installed K40. The original atlas graph called J6 `X` and J7 `Y`; both current manufacturer installation guides contradict that association, so the new drawing corrects the labels without altering the retained graph or historical figures.

| Type 2 laser PSU low-voltage terminal | 1 `G`, 2 `IN`, 3 `5V` | Left-to-right in the Type 2 manufacturer's drawing. `IN` is the former controller PWM input. The Mini Gerbil cards describe the **existing controller** and its after-driver outputs. They do not supply a known adapter between SmoothieBox and the actual motors/endstops. The Type 2 PSU `IN` is a plausible *function* for a future SmoothieBox laser-control design, but the voltage, grounding, interlock, and configured output are not established, so this atlas selects **no wire**. No Type 1 PSU contact bank is invented for `laserplot-01`. All represented contacts stay OPEN; this is not a wiring approval.
