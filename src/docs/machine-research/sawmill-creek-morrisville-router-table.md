# Morrisville College student-built 3-axis CNC router table

**Machine identity:** a custom 3-axis CNC router table designed and built by a group of Morrisville College students, reported by their adviser Bill Griggs on Sawmill Creek. It is distinct from the adviser’s own routers.  
**Evidence state:** the adviser reported a completed machine and posted a “first cut” video reference on 25 May 2012. The thread establishes construction and an initial cutting demonstration, but does not document later service history.  
**Novelty check:** searches of `src/docs/` and `docs/` text on 2026-09-23 for “Morrisville”, the thread identifier, and the distinctive 54 × 61 in work envelope found no match outside this research directory. This is a text search; similarly sized routers may exist elsewhere under different names.

## Project and attribution

Bill Griggs says he was asked to advise a student group and describes the project as successful. The machine itself was designed and built by the students; Griggs says his role included advising on axes, reviewing purchases, sharing a pre-release version of his Warp Drive Z-axis plans, and letting the students study his modified Joe's CNC router. Those references are design lineage/context, not proof that this is Griggs's own machine or a stock Joe's CNC build.

The students' router is reported as a three-axis machine made from aluminum and steel, with a 54 × 61 × 6 in cutting area (approximately 1,372 × 1,549 × 152 mm). The thread describes a welded base, a welded full-length contact surface instead of suspended rails, dual 8020 X-axis members, a trammable head, and a spoilboard. The adviser says the students developed a new machine with features informed by studied plans.

The adviser notes that the class had access to Haas machining centers, Bridgeport mills, rapid-prototyping machines, and multiple workshops. This helps explain that the machine was built in an educational manufacturing environment, not necessarily with typical hobby-shop constraints.

## Control and software
