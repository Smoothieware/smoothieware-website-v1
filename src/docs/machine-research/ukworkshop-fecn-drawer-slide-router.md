## Construction and motion

Fecn describes making a CNC router from MDF and repurposed heavy-duty server-rack slides. The build used threaded rod identified in the forum as M8 × 1.5 and bearings. The builder says he used three drawer slides per axis orientation to reduce play. The posts describe stepper motors and drivers but do not identify their models, the exact slide model, full travel, or measured stiffness and backlash.

The machine was controlled with Mach3 during the initial build discussion; a later post refers to Mach2 in the toolpath workflow. The thread therefore records a software change or correction in the described workflow, but does not provide enough detail to determine the exact software/version history.

Fecn initially used a Proxxon tool as the spindle. The thread does not establish whether it remained installed for later work, nor does it give a spindle-speed range, collet, tool model, or electrical interface.

## Reported use

On 29 September 2007, the owner described the machine moving and making its first cut. A follow-up reports cutting a square hole and says workpieces of approximately 700 × 500 × 150 mm could fit. These are owner-reported dimensions and early-use observations, not surveyed travel or accuracy measurements.

In a post dated 30 September 2007, Fecn says he surfaced the spoilboard and then carved a cat from 6 mm plywood in three passes, using artwork converted through CorelDraw, DXF, and Mach2 G-code. He reports that fine details broke away. This records an actual reported carve and its limitation; no feed rate, cutter geometry, dimensional accuracy, or repeatability test is given.

The lightweight MDF-and-drawer-slide construction should not be treated as a rigid metal-frame design. The thread does not establish current condition or long-term service life.
