# Beovoxo's Deckel FP4A LinuxCNC retrofit

## Machine and retrofit state

LinuxCNC Forum member Beovoxo reports acquiring a used Deckel FP4A mill and planning a full EtherCAT servo retrofit. The original control unit and electrical cabinet were removed. The owner installed LinuxCNC on an IBM M78 computer and later reported getting a Beckhoff EK1100 running under LinuxCNC EtherCAT. By February 2025, the plan was for the EtherCAT I/O to control transmission functions, while the CX unit might serve as a conventional PLC. The inspected thread does not establish that the mill was fully commissioned.

A separate respondent identified owning the same model FP4A with a Heidenhain TNC355; that is not Beovoxo's machine. Advice in the thread about other FP4A configurations is kept distinct from this owner's build.

## I/O and connector evidence

The forum thread discusses planned Beckhoff EtherCAT I/O, gearbox/transmission control, and LinuxCNC integration, but does not provide a verified contact-level wiring map for this machine. Mentions of related Deckel machines and their lubrication or gearbox signals are not transferable pin assignments.
