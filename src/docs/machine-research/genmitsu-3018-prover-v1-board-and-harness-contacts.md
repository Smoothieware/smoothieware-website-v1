The manual inventory identifies the supplied controller as **GC3D3** and its printed pages 21–28 identify the machine wiring. The assembly guide independently follows the first-generation board. These sources document a supplied first-generation build, not the revision and continuity of any particular installed machine. The guide's parts list says four limit switches and accidentally lists Z-LIM− twice; its wiring text and manual both instruct six separate X±, Y±, Z± switch cables. The six-cable set is the better-supported reference.

## Contact evidence for the new SVG

| Interface | Count | Source and interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| X, Y, Z motor board sockets | 4 each | Manual printed pp. 21 and 26 and assembly §3.6 show separate four-conductor motor cables. The board photo shows four socket positions for each axis; B−/B+/A−/A+ silk appears beside the sockets, but an installed motor-end phase and cable continuity have not been measured. These are **former-controller winding outputs after on-board drivers**, never STEP/DIR inputs. The SVG enumerates board-view positions without mapping phases. |
| X+, X−, Y+, Y−, Z+, Z− limit board sockets | 3 each | Manual printed pp. 21–25 and assembly §§3.3–3.5 show six distinct labeled three-conductor limit cables. The assembly photos show red/black/white plugs. The source does not assign each cavity to signal, supply, or return. The SVG enumerates positions, not mating-face pin numbers; none is given a guessed electrical role. |
| Spindle motor conductors | 2 | Manual printed p. 27 and assembly §3.7 identify red to motor M+ and black to M−. These are motor power leads; no direct SmoothieBox logic/power output is assigned. The manual text accidentally says “Z axis motor” in this spindle section; the section title and red/black M+/M− photo establish that it means the spindle. |
| E-stop board input | 2 | Manual printed p. 28 instructs removing the shorting jumper in the EStop socket and inserting the button plug; the two jumper positions are enumerated. Neither contact polarity nor safety rating is specified. The factory button function does not establish a safety-rated SmoothieBox emergency-stop circuit. |
| Z-probe board input | 2 | The manual printed p. 21 shows a separate PROBE socket and a two-lead tool/plate kit. The board-view positions are not assigned polarity, connector cavity number, or SmoothieBox route. |
| Offline controller | 10 | Manual printed p. 21 and the assembly board photo show an IDC-style accessory socket. SainSmart's compatibility guide identifies a 10-pin JTAG cable for 3018 PROVer. Per-contact signal roles and orientation remain unknown; this is an accessory of the former controller, not a SmoothieBox port. |
| Optional laser supply and PWM outputs | 2 + 2 | Manual printed pp. 21–22 labels `-LAS+` as 12 V module power and `-PWM+` as laser PWM output. These are separate former-controller two-position outputs, not one interchangeable module connector. A laser is not in the machine inventory; the ports are optional board references, OPEN. |
| Optional 0–5 V spindle-controller signal | 2 | Manual printed pp. 21–22 labels a two-contact `GND 0-5V` output for a separate spindle controller. No such controller is proved fitted on this default DC-spindle machine. Its former-controller output is OPEN. |

The manual's 24 V 5 A power supply, barrel jack, USB port, physical switch, and driver electronics are not assigned contact schedules: the source does not provide safe individual cavity functions or these are former-controller internals. The old board's motor winding outputs must not be wired to SmoothieBox STEP/DIR pins. A replacement driver stage, motor coil continuity, limits' signal/return map, spindle drive and emergency-stop design all need exact fitted hardware evidence before any solid wire can be drawn. All 52 selected source positions remain **REFERENCE ONLY / OPEN**, with zero SmoothieBox routes or dotted guesses.
