# Richard Gonzalez's custom 49 × 60 in CNC router

**Machine identity:** A custom router designed and built by Sawmill Creek member Richard Gonzalez. The owner explicitly describes it as his own design, not a kit. It is a specific machine, distinct from the Morrisville student router and the separate family-built routers discussed in adjacent posts.  
**Evidence state:** Gonzalez reported using the machine for cutting and described extended operation at high feed rates in July 2018. These are owner statements; no independent measurements or present-day condition are established.  
**Novelty check:** A repository text search on 2026-09-23 for “Richard Gonzalez”, “49×60”, “49 x 60”, and the thread title found no match outside `src/docs/machine-research/`. This does not rule out similar machines described under other names.

## Build and configuration

Gonzalez describes a 49 × 60 in cutting area (about 1,245 × 1,524 mm) and an overall machine weight of roughly 500 lb. He says the design was his own and that he used 8020 aluminum extrusion bought as a bargain. A related Sawmill Creek build discussion says he wanted the machine to accommodate indexed 4 × 8 ft sheet work; this is design context, not proof of a full-sheet cutting envelope.

The machine uses a 2.2 kW water-cooled spindle and Mach4 on a dedicated computer. Gonzalez reports 600 in/min rapids and says he cuts at over 200 in/min for long work sessions. Those speeds and the “all day” operating description are owner-reported; the thread does not provide instrumented accuracy, repeatability, or duty-cycle measurements.

The forum posts do not identify the motion controller, motor or driver models, power supplies, axis transmission, spindle VFD model, toolholding, or the machine's full mechanical drawings. Do not infer these from the design family or photographs.
