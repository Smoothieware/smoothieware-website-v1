# Sienci LongMill MK2 / LongBoard source boundary

Checked 2026-09-26 for atlas profile `parent-sienci-longboard`.

- [Sienci LongBoard Details](https://resources.sienci.com/view/lmk2-longboard-details/) identifies the MK2-era LongBoard as an Arduino Uno and four-TB6600-driver controller. It describes four four-wire motor connections (X, Y1, Y2, Z), up to three pairs of two-wire limit switches, one two-wire probe, and separate spindle PWM/direction signals. Its connector list gives housing types and position counts; it does not assign every machine-cable conductor to a connector cavity or motor coil.
- [Sienci MK2 Open Source](https://resources.sienci.com/view/lmk2-open-source/) distinguishes MK2 LongBoard from the later MK2.5 SuperLongBoard and says the MK2 LongBoard design continues from revision 1.4.3. Its linked `Sienci-LongBoard-Rev-1.4.3-1.zip` (download SHA-256 `5205861e762a9ddde914bc110b5173d6cd312a5077882a30a14f545b56ec7270`) contains 114 archive entries under a directory named `Sienci LongBoard Rev 1.4.3`; the project and schematic files inside are titled `TB6600 Driver Carrier`. This archive as currently served is not a complete MK2 LongBoard machine-cable pin table.
- The formerly linked [32-bit schematic PDF](https://resources.sienci.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Longboard_32bit_ext_Schematic_B9_FULL_PLACE-9-1.pdf) has printed title `Sienci Altmill 32-bit CNC Controller`, revision B9, 27 pages (download SHA-256 `8a8a9f7057771815b7cd516519ee978aaf6d99638affca5378d1f0504ae2d07f`). Its connector labels and numbers must not be imported into the MK2 LongBoard profile.

The current atlas graph names seven MK2 LongBoard groups: X, Y1, Y2 and Z motor paths, limits, probe, and SpinPWM. It gives no source-scoped connector-cavity numbers. Keep these OPEN group cards until an applicable MK2 harness assignment is recovered. The manufacturer's generic four-wire/two-wire descriptions do not establish conductor order, motor phase polarity, limit switch contact choice, or a fitted SmoothieBox cable.
