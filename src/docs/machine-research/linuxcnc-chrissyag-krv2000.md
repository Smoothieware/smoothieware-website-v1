# ChrissyAG's KRV2000 CNC-conversion mill

## Machine and conversion state

LinuxCNC Forum member ChrissyAG reports that they purchased a 1996 KRV2000 milling machine in 2020 for a CNC retrofit intended for disabled use. They had considered but rejected converting their earlier RF45-clone Chester Superlux mill because of its limited travel and rigidity. The KRV2000's electronics were initially on a test bench; the owner reports LinuxCNC 2.8 controlling spindle VFD, servo motors, and limit switches successfully during setup. The original Huangyang VFD was replaced in the test configuration with a Nflixin 9100 producing 380 V three-phase output, while the Huangyang VFD was used to keep the mill running manually during fabrication. The inspected search-indexed forum post does not establish final installation or commissioning.

The owners describe Lichuan servos, Debian 9, and the Probe Basic interface. Their report concerns the KRV2000 retrofit; details of their previous RF45-clone are not included as a second machine dossier here.

## I/O and connector evidence

The owner reports functional spindle-VFD, servo, and limit-switch control on the test bench, but does not give a connector-view or terminal-by-terminal map. No contact numbers, electrical levels, or complete harness assignments are established by the inspected forum post.
