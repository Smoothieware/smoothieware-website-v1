# Bigfoot16's LowRider 4 in northeast Wyoming

**Machine identity:** Dan Harley (`Bigfoot16`)'s individual LowRider 4 CNC router build, documented in V1E's Your Builds forum. It is an owner-specific build of the shared LR4 design, not a separate model.  
**Evidence state:** Harley reported first cuts on 17 January 2025, described the LR4 as complete on 28 January, measured a 1 mm difference between 1200 mm and 2550 mm diagonals the next day, and later reported a Fluid Dial pendant working.  
**Novelty check:** A repository search on 2026-09-23 for `Bigfoot16`, `LR4 in NE WYOMING`, and the thread identifier found no match in the existing docs or atlas. This verifies a distinct build record, not a unique machine design.

## Build and setup

Harley says he assembled the machine and made the first strut-plate cuts before tidying the wiring and dust hose or checking square and tram. On 28 January he said the LR4 was complete and had fitted a prototype drag-chain mount; he planned to check square and tram before starting further cuts. On 29 January he reports checking the diagonals, with 1200 mm and 2550 mm measurements differing by 1 mm. This is one owner measurement, not a calibrated accuracy test.

In March, the owner said he had a Fluid Dial pendant working. He later added printed Z blocks to hold the gantry up, noting that they address Z drop after power-off. The thread does not identify the controller board, motors, drivers, power supply, spindle/router, cutting area, or wiring scheme.

## Reported operation

The opening post says the first cuts were the machine's strut plates and calls them successful. By late January the owner considered the build complete but still planned geometry checks before routine use. The post does not specify the material, cutter, feed, depth per pass, or job file for the strut-plate cuts. Do not infer these settings from the pictures.
