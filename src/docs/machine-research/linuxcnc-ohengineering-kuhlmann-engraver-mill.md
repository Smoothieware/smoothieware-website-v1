# OHEngineering's Kuhlmann engraver/mill retrofit project

## Machine identity and present project stage

LinuxCNC Forum member OHEngineering reports buying a Kuhlmann engraver/mill at auction in 2025 for less than €50 including fees and tax. The owner says its controls were partly missing and outdated. The installed system is identified as a Berger Lahr KIM500, and the original machine had five-phase stepper motors; the owner says all of the stepper drivers were missing. The exact Kuhlmann model and machine serial number are not stated in the inspected thread.

This is an ongoing retrofit project, not a completed LinuxCNC installation. The owner initially planned to replace the stepper system with servos and EtherCAT I/O, and later reported already having EtherCAT servos and I/O. In January 2026 the machine had been disassembled for cleaning and was mostly reassembled. Motor adapter plates and a full set of electrical schematics were still planned; the owner said the main electrical layout had been sketched but still needed details and schematics.

The owner reports that a separate attempt with TB6600 drives had missed steps and failed parts, and that they had used DM556-series drives with better results on their other setup. That earlier statement does not establish that either drive type was installed on the Kuhlmann machine. The retrofit's eventual drive, I/O, and controller configuration remains unreported in the inspected posts.

## Evidence and limits

The forum post includes photographs of the machine as acquired, its Berger Lahr KIM500 control, removed electrical parts, and the cleaning/reassembly work. The photos document project condition and progress, not a wiring diagram. The thread does not provide a machine connector map, axis pinout, terminal assignments, servo or EtherCAT device models, field wiring, or an as-built schematic.

The owner's statements establish the planned and reported project state as of 2026-01-21 only. They do not establish that the retrofit was completed or that the machine ran under LinuxCNC. Revisit the forum thread before using this as a current project-status record.
