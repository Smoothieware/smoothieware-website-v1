# dextaca's Citizen Cincom L16 retrofit proposal

## Machine and project state

LinuxCNC Forum member dextaca identifies a Swiss Cincom Citizen L16 and asks about a prospective LinuxCNC retrofit. The proposed initial configuration was one spindle and three axes, with a future sub-spindle configuration of one spindle plus two axes. The owner was considering Yaskawa servos in analog mode. The inspected thread contains feasibility discussion but no evidence that a retrofit was started or installed.

This dossier records a specific intended project around an identified machine, not a completed retrofit. The forum respondents' general suggestions about Mesa and other retrofits are not evidence of hardware installed on this L16.

## I/O and connector evidence

No L16 connector, drive, encoder, I/O, terminal, or contact assignment is established by the inspected thread. Generic Mesa channel capacities mentioned in a reply are product-level discussion and do not form a machine pinout.
