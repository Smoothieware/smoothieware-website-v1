# RotarySMP's 7×12 mini-lathe rebuild and LinuxCNC conversion

## Machine and retrofit state

LinuxCNC Forum member RotarySMP describes rebuilding a specific 7×12 mini-lathe and preparing it for LinuxCNC. The machine had previously run TurboCNC with a once-per-revolution spindle encoder, but the owner noted that its X-axis ballscrew had never been installed. After the plastic back gears broke and its DC motor failed, the spindle was changed to a three-phase 3/4 hp induction motor and VFD with a toothed-belt drive. The owner also undertook scraping and geometry correction of the bed and tailstock surfaces.

The intended CNC scope was X and Z axes plus VFD spindle control; the toolpost and tailstock were to remain manual. A Mesa 7i96, small Gigabyte computer, and 10-inch touchscreen were described as planned/inbound early in the thread. The inspected page documents rebuild progress and attached wiring/HAL/INI materials but does not establish that this LinuxCNC conversion was completed or commissioned.

## I/O and connector evidence

The forum page says a wiring diagram, HAL, and INI were attached, but the attachment contents were not inspected here. No connector positions or complete contact assignments are transcribed. Machine functions mentioned in prose must not be mistaken for a physical pinout.
