# OpenBuilds ACRO + BlackBox: optional kit peripheral contacts

**Atlas scope:** `parent-acro-blackbox`, the OpenBuilds ACRO maker build with an optional BlackBox controller. The [OpenBuilds ACRO build and parts list](https://builds.openbuilds.com/builds/openbuilds-acro-system.5416/) describe an XY system, three optional NEMA 17 motors, two Xtension limit switch kits, and an optional BlackBox. The source does not fix a BlackBox 4X/X32 generation, identify an installed example's actual bundle selections, or fix a laser/pen tool. Its controller alternatives and generic xPRO Z-axis wording must not become a fitted ACRO Z motor.

The ACRO's three motor locations are X, left Y and right Y; the latter two share the Y movement in the maker's controller guidance. The parts list supplies the *count* of three optional OpenBuilds NEMA 17 motors. OpenBuilds' [BlackBox 4X NEMA17 guide](https://github.com/OpenBuilds/docs-migrated/wiki/docs_blackbox-4x_connect-nema17) shows the OpenBuilds NEMA17 motor pigtail with four red, blue, green and yellow conductors in its [motor drawing](https://github.com/OpenBuilds/docs-migrated/wiki/media/nema17.png), captured SHA-256 `288e49423e71464f0cf550a6a164aec21047ce9a1fde4b7420e8a7b6b2b088e2`. The guide explicitly restricts those color codes to OpenBuilds motors. For each ACRO kit-option motor, the atlas records four **color-identified reference leads**, not cavity numbers, mounted motor coil measurements, or a BlackBox output pinout. Controller-side Y2 winding order and actual motor direction remain unverified.

OpenBuilds' [Xtension switch guide](https://github.com/OpenBuilds/docs-migrated/wiki/docs_xtensions_limits) says the Xtension limit switch has a three-position connector. Its [switch product image](https://github.com/OpenBuilds/docs-migrated/wiki/media/xtensionlimit.png), captured SHA-256 `a9d629ad4190602b27d279e948f01d317dd0eb916cb1b8fd1ff83b139c56fc44`, visibly marks the three switch-side contacts `GND`, `V+`, `SIG` adjacent to the housing. The ACRO parts list counts two optional switches, but does not establish which one is X/Y, its physical installation or electrical compatibility with SmoothieBox. The atlas keeps them as switch A/B, both **REFERENCE ONLY / OPEN**.

The resulting five optional machine-peripheral cards contain 18 reference contacts: 12 motor pigtail leads and six switch-side contacts. The former BlackBox controller remains an unresolved interface, **not** a motor driver that can receive SmoothieBox STEP/DIR at its motor-output terminals. The machine's laser or pen is an option with no fitted product/connector in the cited ACRO build; no tool-control pin is assigned.
