# Genmitsu 3030-PROVer MAX spindle and optional laser leads

Checked 2026-09-26 against the [Genmitsu 3030-PROVer MAX User Manual v1.0 (July 2023)](https://genmitsu.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/101-60-3030PVM/Genmitsu_3030_PROVer_MAX_Manual_V1.0_202307.pdf) and SainSmart's [CNC Laser Module Installation Guide](https://docs.sainsmart.com/article/1z4ikdh3rb-cnc-and-laser-module-installation-guide). The downloaded manual has SHA-256 `30ad5817b1ccdf87c6fdbce707f31932e4a231977a04db618b5ae064b593d188` and 101 PDF pages.

The manual's wiring chapter, printed page 20 (PDF page 22), identifies the spindle motor's **red positive lead** and **black negative lead** and shows them joined to matching power-cable conductors. The specification section gives a 48 V DC, 300 W spindle. These are two named conductors, not numbered connector cavities; the join is on the spindle power path, not a SmoothieBox logic output. Installed spindle-drive/PSU details, current handling, protection, switching and electrical fit must be determined separately. Keep both leads OPEN in the atlas.

The SainSmart laser compatibility table groups the **3030-PROVer MAX** with other named models and explicitly lists an optional, package-supplied **3-pin laser connector cable in 12 V, GND, PWM order**, with no extra power supply required for that listed option. Treat the table order as a source contact sequence, not a proven view of either mating face. The laser is an **option**, not evidence that every 3030-PROVer MAX has one. The exact fitted laser module, current draw, enable behavior, PWM polarity and SmoothieBox interface are unverified. Keep all three positions OPEN and separate from the spindle power path.

The manual's printed pages 18–21 (PDF pages 20–23) also name A Axis, Air Pump, Laser Module, Offline Controller, Y/E/P, Handwheel, USB and three keyed offline-controller cables with 3, 6 and 14 positions. The power-box cable has one eight-position terminal row at each end. The manual does **not** assign the individual functions of those 3/6/14- or eight-position connectors. Preserve them as named OPEN groups; do not manufacture per-contact functions or equate the Y/E/P group with a fully known 14-pin pinout.
