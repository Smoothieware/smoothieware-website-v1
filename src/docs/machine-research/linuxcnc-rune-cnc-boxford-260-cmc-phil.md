# Rune_cnc's Boxford 260 CMC training mill “Phil”

## Machine and retrofit state

LinuxCNC Forum member Rune_cnc describes restoring a Boxford 260 CMC training machine nicknamed “Phil,” which had been unused for six years at a school in Norway. In December 2024, the owner said the machine had been got working again and that a Mesa card was being installed as a replacement controller. A later post said safety additions, including an E-stop and limit sensors, still needed to be added. The inspected thread does not establish completed commissioning with LinuxCNC or the status of those safety changes.

This is distinct from the Boxford 190VMC and lathe projects documented elsewhere. It is a mill, and the owner-provided history and nickname identify this individual machine.

## I/O and connector evidence

The thread says the replacement will use a Mesa card and mentions outstanding E-stop and limit-sensor work, but gives no selected card model, connector view, signal levels, contact assignments, or completed wiring. No pinout can be inferred from the forum discussion.
