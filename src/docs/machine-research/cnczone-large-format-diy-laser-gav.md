# Gav's large-format DIY CO₂ laser cutter

**Machine class:** custom large-format laser-cutter build documented in CNCArena/CNCzone's Laser Material Processing forum. The forum title does not provide a model number; this dossier uses the builder/thread identity to avoid inventing one.  
**Evidence state:** design and component claims from the initial build thread, plus forum critique and correction. Machine completion and final configuration are not proven by the reviewed posts.  
**Novelty check:** thread/build identity not found in the current machine-control survey HTML or sibling assets on 2026-09-23.

## Forum-reported design

The builder calls this a second laser-cutter attempt, after building an early Buildlog.net design. The author reports about one month of design/build time and an estimated £1,500–£2,000 cost. At the thread's opening, the builder describes it as a 40 W machine and says a LightObject DSP would control the laser and steppers; upgrading to 80 W or 100 W was only a possibility.

A responder disputes the tube's stated power based on its visible/estimated length and challenges the axis-rod size and direct stepper drive arrangement. The builder accepts that the Y drive shafts and transmission should be revisited and mentions possible Panasonic servo motors. Other participants disagree with the responder's tube-power inference and discuss tube length/output variability. Therefore, tube identity and power are **contested/unknown**, not settled as 40 W, 80 W, or 100 W.

The thread's image attachments show the machine during the early build, but the images are hosted through third-party image links and were not recovered as stable forum attachments in this pass. The source forum text does not provide a terminal-level controller-to-laser pinout.
