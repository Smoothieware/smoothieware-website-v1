## Build and reported operation

The opening post identifies a large-format DIY CO₂ laser cutter, with a 20 × 30 inch intended format and model aircraft as the use case. On 2022-12-04 the owner reported first test cuts and remaining firmware adjustments. On 2022-12-05 the owner posted two machine photos and a first-cut result, saying they did not yet know what was wrong with the result. Suggestions from other forum members include possible backlash and possible gantry/mirror alignment effects; those are replies and hypotheses, not owner-confirmed causes or completed repairs.

The posts inspected do not identify the tube wattage, laser power supply, controller, firmware, motion electronics, cutting material, speed, or tested work envelope. Do not infer any of these from the pictures or from another user's discussion of their own laser.
