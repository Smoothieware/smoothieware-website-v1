# CCCHH K40 “Major Lazer”

## Identity

The CCCHH wiki describes its “Major Lazer” as a K40 40 W CO₂ laser engraving/cutting machine. The page describes this installation, not all machines sold under the K40 name.

## Wiki evidence

- [CCCHH machine page](https://wiki.hamburg.ccc.de/item:lasercutter): installation history, operation page links, machine connection workflow, material database and maintenance sections.
- [CCCHH K40 installation instructions (wiki attachment)](https://wiki.hamburg.ccc.de/_media/item%3Ak40_installation_instructions.pdf): the panel diagram labels main power, laser enable, test, current adjustment and current indication. It says operating current should remain below 15 mA and alignment below 10 mA; verify these instructions against the installed tube/controller before use.

## Operation and safety

The wiki explicitly says never to operate the machine unattended and explains that its software has a pause control. The attached guide describes homing on power-up and control-panel use. These are source-reported instructions, not a substitute for current training or inspection.

## Pinout and diagrams

The attachment contains a control-panel illustration, but no numbered controller connector pinout was found. Its panel labels are transcribed above; no internal electrical connections are inferred.

## Identity caveat

This installation name is “Major Lazer”; the exact OEM chassis revision is not stated. “K40” is a broad machine class, so do not merge it with another K40 installation without matching source evidence.
