# Tormach 8L CNC lathe — Dallas Makerspace

**Wiki evidence status:** dedicated local wiki page with machine specifications, operation notes, training context, and CAM guidance. It does not provide a wiring or connector pinout.

## Machine and local configuration

The Dallas Makerspace page identifies a Tormach 8L CNC lathe. It lists 4.5 in X travel and 10 in Z travel with tailstock, 8 in maximum swing over bed, 4 in over carriage, 1.5 hp spindle power, and spindle speeds from 180 to 5,000 rpm across two ranges. The listed supply is single-phase 115 VAC, 50/60 Hz, on a 15 A breaker. It names high-performance polyphase stepper motors with Leadshine microstepping drivers on X and Z.

Accessories listed for this installation include a 4 in four-jaw chuck with 5C mount, an OXA quick-change tool post and holders, and a post-mounted knurling tool.

## Operating and CAM notes

The wiki directs new users to learn with a virtual PathPilot controller instance. It records facing-operation DRO sign conventions for front- and rear-mounted tools. Users who CAM externally are directed to the 8L Fusion 360 post-processor on the makerspace training drive; the wiki warns that the 15L post-processor is incompatible because the 8L post's default X-axis direction is reversed relative to the 15L.
