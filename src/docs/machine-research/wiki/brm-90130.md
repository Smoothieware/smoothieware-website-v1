# BRM 90130 laser cutter

## Identity

Attraktor's wiki identifies its CO₂ laser as a BRM 90130. The page gives conflicting tube ratings: its summary says 100 W while its operational description says 150 W. Treat installed power as unresolved until the machine label is checked. This record covers the specific Attraktor installation.

## Wiki evidence

- [Attraktor Lasercutter](https://wiki.attraktor.org/Lasercutter): exact manufacturer/model, reported work area 1280 × 850 mm, DXF and LightBurn/LaserWork workflow, member training/tutor restrictions, and laser safety guidance.
- [Attraktor BRM operation manual attachment](https://wiki.attraktor.org/images/5/59/BRM_90130_Gebrauchsanleitung.pdf): wiki-hosted manual reference.

The wiki requires training, initial supervised operation and an unattended-fire precaution. Follow the current local procedure and keep the machine observed.

## Pinout and diagrams

No electrical connector pinout is published on the cited wiki page. Its linked machine image/manual may show controls, but that is not evidence of controller I/O assignments. Do not infer wiring from the model name.

## Additional model-manual evidence checked

The [BRM 90130 Version 2 operating manual](https://wiki.attraktor.org/images/5/59/BRM_90130_Gebrauchsanleitung.pdf) is dated 2016-04-09 and includes a sample machine built 2015-07-20, serial 91513909201. The serial-specific data identifies that sample as 100 W; it does **not** resolve the Attraktor unit's conflicting 100 W/150 W statements unless its nameplate matches. The same manual includes tube-current reference pages for 80 W, 100 W, 130 W and 150 W options, so the model family includes multiple laser configurations.

The manual's table of contents calls the electrical schematic page 120, and the PDF labels it page 120, but its text says additional schematics are available on request; no usable terminal assignments were recovered from that page. Treat these as **model-manual connector-group and functional relationships**, not a pinout for the specific Attraktor installation. The manual itself records one sample serial and revision scope; compare it to the local machine before transferring ratings or connectors.
