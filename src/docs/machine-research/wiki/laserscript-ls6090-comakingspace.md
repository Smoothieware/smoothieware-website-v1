# HPC LaserScript LS6090 — CoMakingSpace configuration

**Wiki evidence status:** named machine configuration with detailed local controls and material notes. The wiki says the supplier was HPC Lasers and rates the installed CO₂ system at 80 W; tube/controller revisions are not recorded.

## Identity and setup

The CoMakingSpace wiki identifies this machine as a LaserScript LS6090 supplied by HPC Lasers. It lists 80 W power, a 600 × 900 mm worktable, and 230 mm Z height. The listed software is LaserCut 5.3, with a laser exhaust system and water chiller. The page's status note says the cutter was not usable until its fire extinguisher had been replaced; this historic wiki status needs current local confirmation before anyone treats the machine as usable.

## Controls and operation

The wiki describes a right-side machine control panel. On startup, the operator presses “Datum” to home the head to the upper-right corner. With no menu item highlighted, direction keys jog the head. Jog travel is configured through Menu → Down → Down → Enter. The page describes focusing with a physical focus gauge on the material while raising the tray until the gauge just slides through. Uploaded jobs are started or paused with “Start / Pause.”

The source identifies LaserCut 5.3 and points to a dedicated wiki material-settings page. It lists exhaust filtration and a water chiller as required associated equipment. The wiki does not supply controller firmware or machine-specific G-code.

## Pinouts and diagrams

No electrical connector, laser power-supply terminal, controller-board identity, or pin assignment appears in the captured wiki evidence. The page shows illustrations of the control panel and a “CANCEL LOGIC ORG” recovery sequence, but these do not establish machine wiring. No electrical pinout has been inferred.
