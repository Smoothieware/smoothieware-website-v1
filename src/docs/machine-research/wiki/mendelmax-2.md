# MendelMax 2.0 RepRap printer

## Identity

MendelMax 2.0 is an aluminium-frame RepRap design with a reduced printed-part count. The wiki says the family later continued as MendelMax 3; this dossier is specifically version 2.

## Wiki evidence

- [RepRap MendelMax 2](https://reprap.org/wiki/MendelMax_2): version identity, dimensions, motion system and specifications.
- [RepRap MendelMax family](https://reprap.org/wiki/MendelMax/fr): family history and MendelMax 1.5/2 context.

## Specifications and operation

The wiki lists a 230 × 310 × 225 mm print area, 450 × 340 × 460 mm overall dimensions, all-aluminium frame and linear rails in X/Y with precision shafting and bushings in Z. The page links build instructions and files. It reports a usable print speed of 150 mm/s or more depending on material and geometry, and warns that exact Z travel depends on the extruder.

## Pinout and visuals

No board revision or connector pinout appears in the captured source. The RepRap wiki page has design images but no verified wiring contact map.

## Limits

The page says MendelMax 2 was succeeded by MendelMax 3. Do not apply later-generation electronics details to this version.
