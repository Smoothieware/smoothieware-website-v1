# Prusa CORE One 3D printer

## Identity

RaumZeitLabor's current wiki homepage names “Prusa Core One” as a 3D printer and lists the hostname `coreone` for the lab's Prusa Core One printer. The spelling varies between “Core One” and “CoreOne.” The lab pages establish identity only; they do not record this unit's electronics revision or connector schedule.

## Wiki evidence

- [RaumZeitLabor homepage](https://wiki.raumzeitlabor.de/wiki/Hauptseite): lists Prusa Core One among lab machines.
- [RaumZeitLabor network inventory](https://wiki.raumzeitlabor.de/wiki/Netzwerk): associates hostname `coreone` with a Prusa Core One 3D printer.

## Manufacturer electronics reference

Prusa's CORE One electronics guide applies to CORE One and CORE One+. It identifies an xBuddy main board, a connected xBuddy extension board, a LoveBoard that interfaces between xBuddy and Nextruder components, and a stepper splitter cable carrying the three Z motors to xBuddy. The guide includes connector-labeled board images and access instructions, but no individual connector contact schedule or signal-to-contact map. These are model-family references only: they do not identify the lab unit's exact board revision, fitted harness, connector mating view, or pin functions. CORE One+ assignments are not transferred.

## Research limits

The local wiki establishes identity and network naming only. The manufacturer guide adds model-family board roles, not an installed wiring map. No revision-matched numbered contact map or electrical specifications suitable for SmoothieBox routing were recovered. Do not infer a contact count or signal function from connector appearance alone.
