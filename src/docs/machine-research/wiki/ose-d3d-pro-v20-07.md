# OSE D3D Pro v20.07 3D printer

## Identity

Open Source Ecology identifies D3D Pro v20.07 as the 8-inch-bed revision using the updated single-piece Universal Axis. It belongs to OSE's modular printer/fabrication family; this record is specific to the v20.07 design page.

## Wiki evidence

- [OSE D3D Pro v20.07](https://wiki.opensourceecology.org/wiki/D3D_Pro_v20.07): bed size, module architecture, scalability and development deliverable status.
- [OSE D3D](https://wiki.opensourceecology.org/wiki/D3D): general modular printer and toolhead context.
- [OSE D3D Pro v20.07 development links](https://wiki.opensourceecology.org/wiki/D3D_Pro_v20.07): links to wiring/plumbing and build-instruction subpages.

## Architecture and operation

The wiki describes five modules: gearless extruder, fast heated bed, Universal Axis motion system, multipurpose Universal Controller and Universal Frame. It says the 8 mm-axis version scales from an 18-inch bed design, with much larger scaling contemplated using a 1-inch axis. The page's development table marks design, electronics, wiring, BOM and build deliverables at 0%; this is an unfinished specification, not proof that the stated build is complete.

## Pinout and visuals

The v20.07 page links a [Wiring and Plumbing work-product page](https://wiki.opensourceecology.org/wiki/D3D_Pro_v20.07_Wiring_and_Plumbing), but its development table records that deliverable at 0%; direct retrieval of the child page returned a cache miss. No controller contact map or physical machine-side terminal schedule was recovered. Do not infer pins from the Universal Controller name or transfer mappings from other D3D generations.

## Status caveat

Treat D3D Pro v20.07 as a documented design snapshot. Its listed “key specification” should not be treated as a validated performance result while the linked development checklist remains incomplete.
