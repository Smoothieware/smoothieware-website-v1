# Creality Ender 3 S1 3D printer

## Identity

Artisans Asylum records the maker as Creality and model as Ender3S1. Its shop category lists two units; operational status varies by the current shop inventory.

## Wiki evidence

- [Artisans Asylum FDM printer category](https://wiki.artisansasylum.com/wiki/Category%3A3D_Printers_-_Extrusion): common use restrictions and thermal/motion safety.
- [Creality Ender-3 S1 download center](https://www.creality.com/download/creality-ender-3-s1-3d-printer): manufacturer lists an Ender-3 S1 user manual dated 2023-08-31; official manual captured from its linked PDF.

## Machine details

The machine page describes a 200 × 200 mm Y-moving bed using rollers on an extruded rail and belt drive; Z uses two screw jacks and two motors; X uses belt-driven rollers. The 1.75 mm extruder has a proximity sensor. The category says nozzle temperature can reach 200–300 °C and the bed about 100 °C, and requires users to stay clear of moving parts.

## Manufacturer manual interface inventory

Creality's Ender-3 S1 User Manual (2023-08-31), §4, labels the mainboard-facing heated-bed thermistor, heated bed, DC 24 V input, fuse, Z-motor interface, filament-sensor interface, Z-limit interface, touchscreen, knob display, mainboard fan, memory-card slot, and Type-C port. Its nozzle diagram labels a 24-position flexible flat cable (FFC), heater cartridge, hotend thermistor, extrusion motor, front-print fan, heatsink fan, and auto-leveling interface. The Z-axis adapter illustration separately labels filament-sensor, +24 V, −24 V, PWM, and Z-motor interfaces. These are manufacturer model-family interface names; the Artisans Asylum page does not identify its installed board, adapter, harness, or subrevision.

The manual's drawings do not provide an individually numbered contact schedule, mating-face orientation, polarity for the interface pins, or breakout mapping from the 24P FFC to the named nozzle devices. Only the 24P cable count is exposed as a numbered count-only schedule; those placeholder positions are not source pin numbers or a pinout. All electrical contacts, board revisions, and wire paths remain OPEN. No SmoothieBox route is asserted.

## Pinout and visuals

No contact-by-contact electrical pinout was found on the cited pages or manufacturer manual. The machine-page image supports model/layout identification only; the manufacturer manual is a family reference, not proof of the local printer's fitted electrical assembly.
