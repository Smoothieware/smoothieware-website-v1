# Do-All 2013-1 metal-cutting vertical bandsaw — Artisans Asylum

**Wiki evidence status:** exact model and local configuration documented. The wiki dates the installed machine to 1977 and describes it as an antique. Its referenced instruction manual is for a similar model; a separate 2013-1 service manual is identified.

## Local configuration and operation

Artisans Asylum identifies a Do-All 2013-1 vertical band saw in its Machine Shop. The wiki says its high-speed range is disabled, leaving only the low range available. The saw is usually set up for mild steel; the page directs users to determine an appropriate blade speed for other materials, then check the feet-per-minute indicator while the saw is running.

Power controls are at the rear, and a work lamp is available. A crank near the lower left changes blade speed and, according to the wiki, must only be turned while the machine is running. A separate rear crank formerly selected low/high range and is clamped out of use. The saw has an integrated spot-welder for joining blade stock; instructions are printed on the welder's front panel.

General metal-cutting vertical-band-saw guidance on the wiki calls for guards in place, the blade guard set close to the work, the work kept flat and secure, cutting oil, and waiting for the blade to stop before removing stock from a partial cut.

## Visuals and pinouts

The article contains local photographs/captions for the rear power switch, speed indicator, speed crank, and blade welder. They document visible machine controls but no wiring or connector assignments. No electrical pinout is published on the inspected wiki pages.
