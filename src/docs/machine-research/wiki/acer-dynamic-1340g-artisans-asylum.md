# Acer Dynamic 1340G manual lathe — Artisans Asylum

**Wiki evidence status:** exact model-specific local wiki page. The page identifies the serial as unknown and has a local user-manual attachment. This is a manual lathe, not a CNC machine.

## Identity and materials

Artisans Asylum identifies the installed machine as an Acer Dynamic 1340G manual lathe. Its wiki page lists mild, alloy, and stainless steel; aluminum, brass, copper alloys, titanium; and PMMA/polypropylene as allowed material classes. It excludes wood, graphite, fibre composites, abrasive additives, and flammable or toxic materials.

## Operating and maintenance information

The inspected wiki page refers users to a locally hosted Acer 1340G manual, a manufacturer manual, and a local copy. It gives lubrication guidance rather than a step-by-step turning procedure. For the apron gearbox, it lists Shell Tonna 33 and several ISO 68 slideway-oil equivalents. For the gearbox and headstock it lists Shell Tellus #27 / renamed Tellus 37, with Tellus S2 MX 32 or MX 46 among the named equivalents. Other oil points are to receive light machining or way oil. The page says to check the oil levels in the sight windows before each use or daily.

## Safety, visuals, and pinouts

The wiki links to its metal-lathe safety notes. The article has no electrical pin map or CNC control information.

The linked six-page owner manual, [ACER 1340G User-man-v4.pdf](https://wiki.artisansasylum.com/images/f/f0/ACER_1340G_User-man-v4.pdf), was inspected. Page 2 names the front-panel coolant-pump switch, jog button, power/emergency-stop button and speed/feed/threading controls. Page 4 labels the apron power-feed, threading and spindle forward/off/reverse lever; page 5 describes the foot pedal as a power-off/brake. These are operator controls and functions, not a numbered electrical connector map. The PDF supplies no machine-side cavity numbers, exposed control terminals, conductor assignments or electrical ratings, so none are drawn as SmoothieBox wiring points.

Acer's [Dynamic-1340G/1440E user-manual listing](https://aceronline.net/support/user-manuals/engine-lathes/dynamic/dynamic-1340g-1440e-user-manual/) and [circuit-diagram listing](https://aceronline.net/support/control-panel-layout/engine-lathes/dynamic/dynamic-1340g-1440e-circuit-diagram/) confirm manufacturer support pages for this model family. The latter currently reports its linked PDF missing. The manual-lathe identity is not evidence of a CNC retrofit, an installed electronic spindle interface, or contact-level machine wiring.
