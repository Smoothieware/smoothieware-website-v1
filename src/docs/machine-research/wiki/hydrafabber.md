# HydraFabber multi-tool CNC prototype

## Identity

Open Source Ecology's wiki describes HydraFabber as a portable, multi-headed CNC fabrication-machine concept intended to combine 3D printing, circuit milling and laser cutting. The wiki says the build was preceded by HydraRaptor and was intended for OSE design sprints; it is a prototype/design, not an established commercial model.

## Wiki evidence

- [OSE HydraFabber](https://wiki.opensourceecology.org/wiki/HydraFabber): intended functions and high-level specifications.
- [OSE Personal Microfactory](https://wiki.opensourceecology.org/wiki/Personal_Microfactory): identifies the related D3D platform and alternative fabrication toolheads.

## Functional information

The wiki sets a minimum 200 × 200 × 120 mm print volume, quick-change heads with repeatable alignment, a suitcase-style travel case and a target setup time under ten minutes. It describes a 1.5 W diode laser and a circuit-mill head with depth control and quick-change PCB holding. A separate OSE microfactory page describes a 4 W diode for a related D3D platform; that different power figure is not silently applied to HydraFabber.

## Pinout and visuals

No controller revision or connector pinout is specified on the HydraFabber page. Its wiki-embedded video and linked equipment page are design/process evidence only; no contact-level wiring diagram was recovered.

## Status and uncertainty

The wiki page itself is an exploratory project record. Requirements do not prove all heads were built or validated. Treat it as a named prototype concept, not a production-ready machine.
