# HPC LaserScript 1290

Research status: wiki-sourced machine dossier; whole-repository deduplication is complete for the exact model string, while visual and electrical evidence remain incomplete. Captured 2026-09-23.

## Identity and status

SoMakeIt’s wiki identifies its laser as manufacturer HPC, model “laserscript 1290,” and elsewhere calls it an “HPC 1290.” Keep both spellings as source labels; do not infer the separate “LS1290 PRO” suffix that appears only in an external datasheet link. The wiki says Southampton University donated the machine. It requires member training and must not be operated unattended.

## Wiki-recorded configuration and workflow

- Cutting area: 1200 × 900 mm, slightly reduced for engraving.
- CO₂ glass tube, water cooled by a compressor-based chiller; exhaust vents outside; air assist is present.
- Wiki-listed engraving speed: up to 400 units/second; the page says faster settings reduce quality, but does not specify the meaning of the speed unit.
- Z table travel/height: 230 mm.
- Computer or USB connectivity; LightBurn is the named software.
- A rotary attachment is recorded. The wiki describes aligning it on bed slots, connecting it to an internal port near the right-hand control panel, re-zeroing axes, setting the Y scale to 195%, enabling rotary mirroring and using 120 lines per inch. These are page-specific setup notes; the source says the “Send” path is recommended because “Start” had not been tested with the rotary.

## Safety and materials

Before use, the wiki requires training, a fire plan, ready fire blanket/extinguisher, clear machine and area, exhaust operation, and use of materials cleared by the space. It says never leave the cutter unattended. It warns against PVC and polycarbonate; material clearance is required for anything not previously assessed. This is a transcription of the space’s wiki guidance, not a universal materials list.
