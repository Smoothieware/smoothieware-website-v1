# HPC LS3060 laser cutter — London Hackspace former machine

**Wiki evidence status:** exact model with local use and material notes. The wiki explicitly says this old cutter was sold and replaced by a Silvertail A0; it is not presented as currently available.

## Identity and specifications

London Hackspace identifies its former machine as an HPC LS3060 laser engraver/cutter with a 300 × 560 mm cutting area. The wiki lists its status as unknown in the metadata, then states in the body that the cutter was sold and replaced by the Silvertail A0. It describes a Coolbot accessory and separate water-cooling system.

## Local operation and material notes

The wiki requires training, a preflight checklist, logging of use, and continuous supervision. It calls for checking the water pump and extractor and monitoring coolant temperature. The historical material list allows specified grades/thicknesses of MDF, plywood, balsa, paper/cardboard, non-PVC foamcore, acrylic and anodized aluminium for engraving, while it prohibits PVC, ABS, fibreglass, glass, circuit boards and polycarbonate. Those notes are historical local guidance and are not a current-material authorization.

## Controller and wiring

The wiki names manuals for the controller and laser hardware and points to information about its controller card, but this research pass uses no linked non-wiki manual page to infer electrical details. No pinout, terminal table or full wiring diagram was captured. The source does not identify the exact controller revision in text.
