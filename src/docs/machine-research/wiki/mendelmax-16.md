# Terawatt MendelMax 1.6

## Identity

The RepRap wiki identifies Terawatt MendelMax 1.6, a metric aluminium-extrusion FFF printer design based on the MendelMax family. It is distinct from MendelMax 2.0 and later revisions.

## Wiki evidence

- [RepRap MendelMax 1.6](https://reprap.org/wiki/MendelMax_1.6): maker, model, dimensions, build envelope, structure and version changes.
- [RepRap MendelMax family](https://reprap.org/wiki/MendelMax/fr): family history.

## Specifications and operation

The wiki lists an approximately 440 × 440 × 410 mm machine envelope, approximately 11 kg gantry mass (excluding motors, PSU and wiring), and a potential 200 × 200 × 200 mm build envelope. It uses 8 mm smooth rails and leadscrews. The article has separate parts and build-instruction sections.

No electronics revision or connector map is documented in the inspected page. Its machine image and printed-part/build sections are mechanical documentation only.
