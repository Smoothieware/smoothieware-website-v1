# 3DPrintMi

## Identity

3DPrintMi is an open-frame RepRap printer design combining a Printrbot/Wallace-inspired layout with a fixed Y gantry. The wiki describes Mini, Plus and Aluminum Plus configurations; these are size/material variants under one design family and are counted as one machine identity here.

## Wiki evidence

- [RepRap 3DPrintMi](https://reprap.org/wiki/3DPrintMi): design, feature set, build variants, specifications, videos and documentation links.
- [RepRap Wallace](https://reprap.org/wiki/Wallace): source page for the related Wallace design.

## Specifications and operation

The wiki lists the Mini at 152 × 152 × 152 mm and the Plus at 200 × 200 × 200 mm. It describes auto-levelling, a blower fan and extruder compatibility with Wade/Bowden or direct drive. Assembly is estimated at 2–3 hours excluding electronics; the wiki provides build manuals and assembly guides. The page reports 80–100 W for the stated hotend/bed conditions.
