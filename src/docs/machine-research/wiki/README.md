# Wiki-only machine research

This folder contains the wiki-only research stream for the machine research task. Its sources are wiki pages and wiki-hosted media only. It does not use external vendor pages, forums, source-code hosts, or non-wiki manuals as factual support.

- [`wiki-registry.md`](wiki-registry.md): 30 distinct wiki sites and verified navigation paths.
- [`source-coverage.md`](source-coverage.md): what was inspected, what yielded machine-specific evidence, and access/source limits.
- [`candidate-index.md`](candidate-index.md): index and evidence-depth classification for every machine dossier.
- [`novelty-audit.md`](novelty-audit.md): current repository-overlap screen, identity exclusions, and limits on the candidate count.
- [`initial-research-report.md`](initial-research-report.md): earlier research handoff, candidate ledger, exclusions, and follow-up leads.

There are currently 125 machine dossier files: 92 use wiki evidence beyond a catalogue-only row, and 33 are explicitly limited to Appropedia catalogue rows. “Dossier” and “candidate” do not mean “new model”: compare identities and aliases with the full repository and other concurrent research streams before counting novelty. Retired machines, community builds, prototypes, and catalogue mentions are called out in their documents. The wiki inventory is finite and enumerated; the survey does not claim every machine page or media item on each site was exhausted.
