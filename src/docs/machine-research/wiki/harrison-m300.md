# Harrison M300 Lathe

Research status: wiki-sourced model dossier. Captured 2026-09-23.

## Identity and training

SoMakeIt identifies an owned Harrison M300 metalworking lathe. The wiki requires induction and says less-experienced operators are supervised until they have sufficient experience for unsupervised use. The source page does not provide a pinout or CNC retrofit evidence; this is a manual lathe.

## Workholding and tooling

The wiki describes a self-centring three-jaw chuck for cylindrical/hex stock; a larger independent four-jaw chuck, adjusted with a dial indicator for concentric work; turning between centres using 60-degree centre holes; and a faceplate as a last-choice setup. It records a Dickson-style quick-change tool post. It distinguishes carbide inserts, high-speed steel, and brazed carbide, with material-dependent considerations; these are the space’s practical notes, not universal cutting parameters.

## Maintenance notes

The wiki says to oil the machine’s oiling points daily, clean swarf/coolant and return tooling after use, check tailstock alignment and oil levels during inspection, and use ISO 68 slideway oil in specified locations. It directs users to check the gearbox-oil viscosity in the machine manual rather than supplying a value.
