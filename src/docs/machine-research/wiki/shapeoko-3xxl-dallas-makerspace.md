# Shapeoko 3XXL — Dallas Makerspace

Research capture: 2026-09-23. Dallas Makerspace documents a Carbide 3D Shapeoko 3XXL in its machine shop for router machining, primarily of thin or flat material. The wiki states that training and instructor sign-off are required.

## Local configuration and use

The wiki lists a DeWalt DWP611 router, belt-driven Z axis, and machining area of 838 × 838 × 76 mm. The supplied collets are 1/4 in and 1/8 in. The table has four parallel T-slot tracks for 1/4 in T-bolts. Users must provide their own sacrificial spoilboard, plan fixtures in advance, and may not screw into the machine spoilboard. At the time of the page's 2021 note, users were expected to bring a computer with Carbide Motion installed; a BitZero is listed for coordinate setting and a Bit Setter was added in 2023 for tool offsets.

The page limits allowed materials to plastics, thin soft metal, and glass only with a diamond-drag bit. It prohibits cutting oil or coolant, fiberglass/composites, and wood except with advance chair permission. As documented when the machine entered service in June 2021, it had no dust collection or enclosure and users were told to follow the cutter with a shop vacuum. Treat that as historical wiki guidance and verify current local rules before use.
