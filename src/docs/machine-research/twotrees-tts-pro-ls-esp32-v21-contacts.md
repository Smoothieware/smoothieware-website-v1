# TwoTrees TTS Pro: LS ESP32 PRO V2.1 reference contacts

## Machine and board scope

- The TwoTrees wiki [TTS-55 Pro](https://wiki.twotrees3d.com/en/LaserEngravingMachine/TTS-55PRO/BriefIntroduction), [TTS-10 Pro](https://wiki.twotrees3d.com/en/LaserEngravingMachine/TTS-10PRO/BriefIntroduction), and [TTS-20 Pro](https://wiki.twotrees3d.com/en/LaserEngravingMachine/TTS-20PRO/BriefIntroduction) introductions identify the MKS LS ESP32 PRO V2.1 board family. The TTS-55/10 introductions state one X motor and two Y motors. TwoTrees' [shared mainboard drawing](https://wiki.twotrees3d.com/en/LaserEngravingMachine/TTS-10PRO/MainBoardDiagram) and its [image](https://oss.twotrees3d.com/LaserEngravingMachine/TTS-10PRO/2.1en.png) label X, Y1, Y2, X/Y limits and laser-head outputs; the equivalent 55/20 wiki pages serve the same image.
- The manufacturer-authored [TTS-55 Pro / TTS-10 Pro manual mirror](https://probots.co.in/technical_data/Two%20Trees%20TTS-55%20Pro%20-%20Manual.pdf.pdf), SHA-256 `d5e1f317e17cf4e6268cdd5c694558aab00429e5965316e95bad1726a2ea0e0f`, English PDF pages 7 and 9, shows X, Y1, Y2 and laser cables and labels the board X/Y1/Y2, X/Y limit and V/G/S laser positions. Its requirements table says TTS-55 Pro **12 V 4 A DC** and TTS-10 Pro **24 V 4 A DC**. The manufacturer-authored [TTS-20 Pro manual mirror](https://f00.psgsm.net/product/928329/twotrees-tts-20-pro-manual.pdf), SHA-256 `41af46024ca84a72f37e25b963f07d8da6f2c7242f15196e35cb9d0f4cabbe06`, English PDF pages 6–7, names X/Y1/Y2 motor cables, X/Y limit cables and laser-module wiring, labels those board positions, and specifies **24 V 4 A DC** input. These manuals do not provide motor cable colours, motor coil assignments or a mating-face cavity view.
- Makerbase's [LS ESP32 PRO V2.1_002 schematic](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/makerbase-mks/MKS-DLC32/main/MKS-ESP32-PRO-main/hardware/LS%20ESP32%20PRO%20V2.1_002/LS%20ESP32%20PRO%20V2.1_002%20SCH.pdf), SHA-256 `83b3c0e9f0a6fc87e59af3cfd7b40163340ef370838d43b91bd01efb7c3df2d3`, dated 2023-11-21, gives **board-reference** contact numbers on sheets 6 (ENDSTOP), 7 (LASER), and 9 (DRIVER). Its [PCB drawing](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/makerbase-mks/MKS-DLC32/main/MKS-ESP32-PRO-main/hardware/LS%20ESP32%20PRO%20V2.1_002/LS%20ESP32%20PRO%20V2.1_002%20PCB.pdf), SHA-256 `653434a1ee2e30cb89ac68bc89571ad864de2750a62d4b8001d9dd282b5fefbf`, locates J21=X, J24=Y1, J23=Y2, J7=X limit, J8=Y limit and the paired J10/J11 laser outputs. The wiki/manual photos say V2.1 but do not prove that every installed board has the `_002` revision; the numbered rows below remain source-revision references until the actual machine is inspected.

## Board-reference positions represented in each primary figure

| Connector | Schematic positions and functions | Installed-machine limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| J21 X motor output | 1 = X driver OUT1B; 2 = OUT1A; 3 = OUT2A; 4 = OUT2B | A board output after its integrated stepper driver, not a STEP/DIR input. Motor cable colour, coil pairing at motor, and mating orientation unverified. |
| J24 Y1 motor output | 1 = Y driver OUT1B; 2 = OUT1A; 3 = OUT2A; 4 = OUT2B | One of two parallel Y sockets. Do not assume the two fitted motors use identical winding orientation. |
| J23 Y2 motor output | Same four Y driver outputs in parallel with J24 | Distinct physical socket and harness; connected motor wire order unverified. |
| J7 X limit | 1 = +5 V, 2 = GND, 3 = X-O signal | Board-input functions, not proof that the fitted limit switch consumes +5 V or of switch polarity. |
| J8 Y limit | 1 = +5 V, 2 = GND, 3 = Y-O signal | Same limit. |
| J11 laser head output | 1 = LC-TTL signal, 2 = GND, 3 = supply rail (schematic net `+12V`) | The manuals mark the laser connection V/G/S, but do not prove the module-side cavity numbering or installed cable continuity. The rail's actual voltage and current rating need model-specific measurement. |

These six installed-function connector cards give **21 individual reference contacts** per profile, all OPEN toward SmoothieBox. The PCB/schematic also show parallel J10 laser terminals and an optional J27 Z driver output. The manuals depict J11's V/G/S laser connection and a manually adjusted Z-height, not a standard installed motorized Z or two simultaneous laser heads; J10 and J27 stay outside the installed-contact inventory. The retained source graph's `Z motor` group is represented by a separate zero-contact **board-option** card so that it cannot be mistaken for a confirmed fitted machine motor.

The schematic's page-3 `+12V` net begins at the 12–24 V board input before the downstream VIN diode; it is not evidence of a regulated 12 V laser output on a 24 V machine. The TTS-10 Pro and TTS-20 Pro manuals specify 24 V machine input. No SmoothieBox 5 V terminal should be treated as a laser supply. The original board's motor outputs likewise cannot receive SmoothieBox STEP/DIR. A retrofit requires separately selected and qualified stepper drivers, laser supply/modulation interface, limit wiring and protection. No physical SmoothieBox route is proven by these sources.
