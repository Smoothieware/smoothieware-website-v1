# Sculpfun S30 and S30 Pro: motherboard contact evidence

## Source scope

- The [Sculpfun S30 series manual](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0628/0695/0066/files/SCULPFUN_S30_Series_User_Manual_2.pdf?v=1780025023), PDF SHA-256 `213f2ee090d7a613caa5cfbc6b62007d257754bda81c940d801d887e28bbc16d`, English pages 8 and 10 (PDF pages 8 and 10), shows separate X and Y motor leads, laser cable, XY limit switches and an air pump. It says X and Y are marked on the motor terminals; it does not give a numbered cable pinout. Its depicted cable placements are assembly guidance, not proof of any SmoothieBox connection.
- The [manufacturer's 32-bit motherboard listing](https://www.sculpfun.com/products/sculpfun-motherboard-32bit) offers an `S6/S9/S10/S30/S30 Pro` variant separately from `S30 Pro Max`. It states that S9, S30 and S30 Pro now use the same motherboard. Its [third product photo](https://www.sculpfun.com/cdn/shop/products/03_5ac7325f-9ed2-47f2-9732-f253b8af9b20.jpg?v=1751621854&width=720) shows a board silk-screened `De By Lv2 2022.08.17`, with a three-contact `Laser` socket (`V+`, `V-`, `PWM`), four-contact `XMOTOR` and `YMOTOR` sockets, two three-contact X/Y limit sockets marked signal/ground/voltage, and a two-contact `Air assist` output marked `+`/`-`. The first and second product photos show the same board at other angles. This is a vendor *product photo* of one revision, not a measured installed-machine harness or connector mating-face view. Contact ordinals introduced below only distinguish visible contacts, not stamped cavity numbers.
- The [S30 5 W machine listing](https://eu.sculpfun.com/products/sculpfun-s30-laser-engraver-machine-5w) lists a 12 V 5 A machine input and 12 V controlled air pump. The [S30 Pro machine listing](https://eu.sculpfun.com/products/sculpfun-s30-pro-laser-engraving-machine-10w) also lists a 12 V pump; its packing list and specifications disagree about whether the included adapter is 5 A or 7 A, so no adapter-current claim is transferred into the drawing. The [S30 Pro laser-module listing](https://es.sculpfun.com/products/s30-pro-modulo-laser-10w) describes a 12 V, 5 A maximum, three-wire module input as `A: PWM+`, `B: GND`, `C: VCC`. These lettered conductors are *module-side descriptions* and are not asserted to be board-socket cavity numbers or a verified installed cable continuity table.
- Sculpfun's [S30 series technical article](https://www.sculpfun.com/blogs/blog/sculpfun-s30-series) warns that the Pro Max uses a different 24 V laser supply even where a board label says 12 V. Therefore the S30/S30 Pro 12 V evidence is **not** applied to `laserplot-24` (Pro Max) without its separate board and module contract.

## Contacts represented in `laserplot-22` and `laserplot-23`

| Manufacturer-photo connector | Individual positions represented | Limit of evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `XMOTOR` | 1, 2, 3, 4: X motor winding conductor, phase unknown | Four visible board-output contacts after the on-board driver. No A/B coil assignment or fitted harness orientation is supplied. |
| `YMOTOR` | 1, 2, 3, 4: Y motor winding conductor, phase unknown | Same limitation. The photo also shows a `YMOTOR2` spare/variant socket; the manual does not establish it as a cabled part of these two standard machines, so it is not normalized into the installed Y harness. |
| X limit socket | `X` signal, `G` ground, `V` accessory supply | Three individual silk-screened board-input contacts. The installed switch's use of `V`, switch logic, and cable continuity are unverified. |
| Y limit socket | `Y` signal, `G` ground, `V` accessory supply | Same limitation. |
| `Laser` | `V+`, `V-`, `PWM` | Board-output contact labels, not a machine-module mating-face map. The S30 Pro module's `A/B/C` names are not silently joined to these labels. |
| `Air assist` | `+`, `-` | Controlled pump **power** output in the photo. S30 machine specification says 12 V pump; output rating, protection and fitted connector orientation still need checking. It is not a SmoothieBox logic input. |

The contact cards are reference views of the *former Sculpfun controller*. SmoothieBox motor STEP/DIR pins must go to compatible external drivers, not the former board's motor outputs. A laser module needs its qualified supply, ground and modulation level; SmoothieBox's 5 V accessory or PWM terminal cannot be presumed to supply the module or pump. No physical SmoothieBox route is selected in this source pass, and no colour-code connection is invented. Both primary figures retain an explicit OPEN boundary for every listed position.
