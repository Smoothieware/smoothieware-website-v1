# Daid Braam's wall-mounted CNC router

**Machine identity:** A custom CNC router designed and built by Maker Forums member Daid Braam, with one Y-axis guide mounted to a wall and the opposite side riding on a table.  
**Evidence state:** The owner reported its first cut on 22 July 2018 and clarified the next day that it had only made one cut so far. This is an early operating milestone, not evidence of established accuracy or reliability.  
**Novelty check:** A repository text search on 2026-09-23 for “Daid Braam”, the thread title, and distinctive 1500 mm / 1200 × 1200 mm dimensions found no match outside this research directory.

## Construction and layout

The owner describes a self-designed machine using OpenBuilds components, 6 mm wooden plates, printed parts, and motors/electronics reused from an Ultimaker 2. The axes are approximately 1500 mm long. The claimed cutting area is up to 1200 × 1200 mm when an extension table is used. The wall-mounted guide and table-supported side are specific to this machine's location; the thread does not provide enough detail to reproduce its structure or establish its rigidity.

The owner estimated around 0.5 mm of error, but explicitly characterized that figure as an unmeasured guess. It is not a calibration result. Firmware identity is not established, and the reused printer electronics do not justify inferring a specific controller or firmware.

## Reported operation

On 22 July 2018, Braam posted that the router had made its first cut. The following day he said that only one cut had been made and described plans to replace the wooden plates with aluminium. The thread therefore establishes a completed machine and one owner-reported cut at that point in time. It does not establish that the planned aluminium upgrade happened or that the machine remains operational.
