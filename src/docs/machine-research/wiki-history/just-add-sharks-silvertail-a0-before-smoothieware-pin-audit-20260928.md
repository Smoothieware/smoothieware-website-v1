# Just Add Sharks Silvertail A0 laser cutter — dossier before Smoothieware pin audit

**Wiki evidence status:** exact machine model independently named by London Hackspace and Bristol Hackspace. London records a 130 W tube and 1200 × 900 mm cutting area. The two wiki pages establish the same machine family, but do not prove identical revisions.

## Identity and operation

London Hackspace identifies “Just Add Sharks Silvertail A0,” purchased by pledge in 2014. Its wiki lists a 1200 × 900 mm cutting area and 130 W tube, requires induction and membership, and stresses continuous supervision because of fire risk. Instructions require a CO2 extinguisher, water pump and extractor, with coolant at 22 ± 3 °C. The operating page describes LightBurn, bed origin and focusing. Bristol Hackspace separately lists the same model family but no Bristol-specific power or work area.

## Pinout and diagram evidence

The London machine page has a “Controller upgrade” heading but no captured connector map. The operating instructions describe bed origin and focal height, not electrical connections. No controller revision, pinout or machine wiring was inferred.

## Supplemental controller and operating-state history

London Hackspace’s [upgrade notes](https://wiki.london.hackspace.org.uk/view/Silvertail_A0_Laser_Cutter/Upgrade_Notes) distinguish stock Lasercut53 from a Smoothieware/LightBurn setup, describe right-rear home and left-front origin, and report an ACnode relay that de-powers the Smoothieboard. The relay contacts, safety-chain topology and board terminals were undocumented. The notes disagree on LCD operation. The main page lists Reci Z2, MYJG-80R, Leetro MPC6515C v2.0 and LaserCut 5.3; variants were to remain separate.
