Seven-channel DC mechanical relay - selected v24 source inspection snapshot

Native source directory:
https://github.com/arthurwolf/extension-boards/tree/main/src/seven-channel-dc-mechanical-relay/attempt/versions/v24

Native CAD, project/rule files and the matching exported BOM and netlist are
retained byte for byte. The schematic declares CERN-OHL-S-2.0; all existing
notices remain in the originals. The licence text is obtained from SPDX:
https://github.com/spdx/license-list-data/blob/main/text/CERN-OHL-S-2.0.txt

Open the .kicad_pcb and .kicad_sch in KiCad 9 for inspection. Their native
footprints/symbol definitions are embedded. This is not the complete original
project tree: the separately referenced symbol/footprint libraries and 3D
models, historical trials and fabrication outputs are not included. Library
resolution and all regeneration require the matching upstream resources.

The file selection and this notice were prepared for the Smoothieware guide
on 2026-10-25; the six original design/export files are unmodified.

v24 is an unqualified TPL7407L prototype study, not a fabrication release.
This snapshot grants no installed load rating, field-use or powered-wiring
acceptance. Consult the matching guide for power, contact and firmware limits.
